It is a purpose-built, rugged EV overlander designed to bridge the gap between zero-emission driving and hardcore, off-grid exploration. ## The Powertrain: Symmetrical AWD Meets Dual-Motor EV Tech

Purists were naturally skeptical when Subaru first dipped its toes into the EV market. However, the 2027 Subaru Uncharted aims to silence the critics by redefining what an electric drivetrain can do off-road. 

Instead of a traditional driveshaft, the Uncharted utilizes a revolutionary dual-motor setup—one on each axle—specifically tuned to mimic and enhance Subaru’s legendary Symmetrical AWD. This system, dubbed **e-Symmetrical AWD**, can distribute torque to individual wheels in milliseconds, far faster than any mechanical differential. 

### Range and Charging Capabilities
One of the biggest hurdles for overland EVs has always been range anxiety. No one wants to get stranded in the backcountry with a dead battery. Subaru addresses this head-on in their 2027 platform by incorporating a next-generation 105 kWh solid-state battery pack. 

*   **Estimated Range:** Up to 420 miles on a single charge.
*   **DC Fast Charging:** 10% to 80% in just 18 minutes using 350kW chargers.
*   **Bi-Directional Charging (V2L):** The Uncharted acts as a giant mobile generator, allowing you to power campsites, electric stoves, or even charge your friends' e-bikes straight from the vehicle's side ports.

## Rugged Design Built for the Trail

Visually, the Uncharted looks like a rugged concept car brought to life. It features an aggressive stance with 10.8 inches of ground clearance—surpassing even the current Outback Wilderness. Heavy-duty underbody skid plates made of lightweight titanium-reinforced composites protect the battery pack from jagged rocks and debris.

The exterior body panels feature a scratch-resistant matte finish, while the wheel arches are lined with durable, recycled cladding. Up top, an integrated, low-profile roof rack system comes standard, capable of supporting up to 800 pounds of static weight—perfect for heavy-duty rooftop tents.

---

## Next-Gen Cabin Tech: Starlink Integration and AR Windscreens

Inside, the 2027 Subaru Uncharted feels like a high-tech basecamp. The centerpiece is a 15.6-inch vertical infotainment screen running Subaru's new Starlink OS 5.0. 

What sets this system apart is its integration with direct satellite communication. Even when you are deep in a canyon with zero cellular reception, the Uncharted can download offline topographical maps, send SOS signals, and receive weather alerts via satellite. 

Furthermore, the windshield features an **Augmented Reality (AR) Head-Up Display**. When navigating tricky off-road trails, the AR system projects virtual lines onto the dirt ahead, showing you the optimal line to take over rocks and ruts, effectively acting as a digital spotter.
