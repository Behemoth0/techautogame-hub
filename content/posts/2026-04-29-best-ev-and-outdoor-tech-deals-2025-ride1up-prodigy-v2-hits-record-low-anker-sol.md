If you follow the e-bike industry, you know that mid-drive motors are the gold standard for performance, torque, and natural ride feel. However, they usually come with a price tag that exceeds $3,000. That is why the latest price drop on the Ride1Up Prodigy V2 is turning so many heads. Now available for just $1,795, this bike represents perhaps the best value-to-performance ratio in the 2025 market.

What makes the Prodigy V2 special? It starts with the Brose TF Sprinter mid-drive motor. Unlike cheaper hub motors found in budget e-bikes, the Brose system is integrated into the pedals, measuring your torque input and providing seamless assistance that feels like you have 'superhuman legs.' It offers 90Nm of torque, which is more than enough to conquer the steepest urban inclines or carry a full load of groceries without breaking a sweat. 

With a Class 3 rating, the Prodigy V2 can hit speeds of up to 28 mph, making it a legitimate car replacement for city dwellers. The V2 update also brought a refined frame design and an integrated battery that keeps the silhouette sleek. At $1,795, you are getting a German-engineered motor and a high-quality components list—including hydraulic disc brakes and a smooth-shifting drivetrain—that usually costs twice as much.
