## Understanding the 2026 ADAS Landscape

When comparing these lease deals, it’s important to look beyond the marketing jargon. Here are the three features you should insist on in 2026:

1.  **Rear Cross-Traffic Braking:** Many cars have alerts, but in 2026, you want a car that will actually apply the brakes if you’re backing out of a driveway into traffic.
2.  **Lane Centering (Not just Lane Keep):** Lane Keep Assist is reactive (bouncing you like a pinball), whereas Lane Centering is proactive, keeping you dead center in the lane. All our picks above feature proactive centering.
3.  **Driver Monitoring Systems (DMS):** With the rise of semi-autonomous driving, a good DMS ensures you stay attentive. Systems using infrared cameras (like Subaru’s DriverFocus) are superior to those that simply measure steering wheel torque.
