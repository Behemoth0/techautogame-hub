## The Ultimate Celebration of Speed in 2025

There is no automotive event on earth quite like the Goodwood Festival of Speed. Part garden party, part high-octane hillclimb, and part international motor show, it has become the premier launchpad for the world's most exciting vehicles. In 2025, the festival grounds are playing host to an extraordinary clash of eras. On one side of the paddock, we have the whisper-quiet, face-melting acceleration of next-generation electric vehicles. On the other, the roaring, mechanical symphony of high-revving internal combustion engines running on sustainable synthetic fuels.

From the lightweight, rally-inspired Alpine A290 to the quad-turbocharged, carbon-fiber-clad Zenvo Aurora, this year's lineup represents the absolute pinnacle of automotive engineering. If you are making the pilgrimage to West Sussex—or watching the live streams from home—here are the standout cars you absolutely must keep your eyes on.

---

## The 5 Standout Stars You Can't Miss

While 28 major new cars are making their dynamic debuts on the hill this year, five specific models represent the past, present, and future of performance. Here is our deep dive into the absolute must-see machinery of 2025.

### 1. Alpine A290
* **Estimated Price:** $46,000 / £36,000
* **Drivetrain:** Single-motor EV (Front-wheel drive)
* **Why it matters:** Alpine is embarking on a bold electric future, and the A290 hot hatch is the opening salvo. Based on the retro-futuristic Renault 5 platform, Alpine’s engineers have widened the track, stiffened the chassis, and added hydraulic bump stops to create a genuine pocket rocket. With up to 217 horsepower in its top-tier GTS trim and a lightweight footprint (for an EV) of under 1,500 kg, the A290 promises to bring pure, chuckable hot-hatch dynamics into the electric era.

### 2. Porsche 911 GTS T-Hybrid (992.2)
* **Estimated Price:** $164,900 / £130,000
* **Drivetrain:** 3.6-liter Single-Turbo Flat-6 with Electric Assist
* **Why it matters:** Purists may have gasped when Porsche announced a hybrid 911, but the 992.2 GTS is not a heavy plug-in hybrid designed solely for tax breaks. This is a performance-oriented system featuring an electric motor integrated into the new eight-speed PDK transmission and an innovative electric turbocharger. The result? A combined output of 534 horsepower, virtually zero turbo lag, and a 0-60 mph time of just 2.9 seconds. It is a masterclass in how to modernize an icon without diluting its soul.

### 3. MG Cyberster GTS
* **Estimated Price:** $76,000 / £60,000
* **Drivetrain:** Dual-motor EV (All-wheel drive)
* **Why it matters:** MG continues its remarkable brand renaissance by bringing a sleek, hardtop coupe version of its Cyberster roadster to the hill. The Cyberster GTS pays homage to the classic MGB GT of the 1960s but packs a modern, dual-motor electric drivetrain producing over 500 horsepower. With its dramatic scissor doors, muscular rear haunches, and a beautifully appointed grand-touring cabin, the GTS proves that electric sports cars can be incredibly desirable.

### 4. Aston Martin Valiant
* **Estimated Price:** $2,500,000 / £2,000,000 (Highly Limited Edition)
* **Drivetrain:** 5.2-liter Twin-Turbo V12 (6-speed Manual)
* **Why it matters:** Originally commissioned as a personal project by Formula 1 legend Fernando Alonso, the Aston Martin Valiant is a brutalist, track-focused evolution of the Valour. It pairs a monstrous 734-horsepower twin-turbo V12 engine with a six-speed manual gearbox—a setup that is vanishingly rare in 2025. Featuring extensive carbon fiber bodywork, 3D-printed subframes, and exotic Multimatic spool-valve dampers, the Valiant is a raw, mechanical masterpiece built for ultimate driver engagement.

### 5. Zenvo Aurora (Agil & Tur)
* **Estimated Price:** $2,800,000 / £2,200,000
* **Drivetrain:** 6.6-liter Quad-Turbo V12 with Triple Electric Motors
* **Why it matters:** Hailing from Denmark, Zenvo has unleashed the Aurora, a hypercar that defies modern conventions. Powered by a bespoke, hot-V 6.6-liter V12 engine that revs to a screaming 9,800 RPM, the Aurora is aided by three electric motors to produce a mind-boggling 1,850 horsepower. The track-focused 'Agil' variant features aggressive active aerodynamics to glue it to the Goodwood tarmac, while the 'Tur' is a sleek, high-speed grand tourer capable of hitting a top speed of 280 mph.

---

## The Battle of Drivetrains: EV vs. Synthetic Fuels

The 2025 Festival of Speed is a fascinating case study in the transition of the automotive industry. On one hand, the paddock is filled with silent, ultra-high-torque electric machines. Brands like Rimac, Lotus, and Polestar are pushing the boundaries of what is possible with battery technology, proving that EVs can conquer the tight, twisting hillclimb course with devastating efficiency.

On the other hand, the roar of internal combustion is far from dead. Manufacturers like Aston Martin, Ferrari, and Porsche are showcasing how high-performance engines can survive—and thrive—using synthetic e-fuels. These carbon-neutral fuels allow classic and modern combustion engines to run without contributing new carbon dioxide to the atmosphere, offering a parallel path to sustainability that preserves the emotional, auditory experience that car enthusiasts love.
