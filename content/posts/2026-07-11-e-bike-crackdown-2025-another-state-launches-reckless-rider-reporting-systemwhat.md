## Introduction

The landscape of urban transportation has transformed rapidly over the last few years. Walk down any major city street, and you will find yourself dodging a silent, fast-moving army of electric bicycles and e-scooters. While these micro-mobility options are fantastic for reducing carbon emissions and bypassing traffic jams, they have also introduced a new brand of chaos to our roads.

In response to mounting public pressure and a spike in pedestrian accidents, another major state has officially introduced a dedicated reporting system targeting reckless e-bike and e-scooter riders. This move, rolling out in early 2025, allows motorists and pedestrians to upload video evidence, note locations, and report dangerous riding behavior directly to local traffic authorities. 

For drivers, this represents a major step forward in road safety and liability protection. For responsible micro-mobility riders, it is a sign that the wild west era of unregulated riding is drawing to a close. Here is everything you need to know about this new initiative, how it impacts your daily commute, and the best gear to protect yourself on the road.

## The Friction Between Cars and Micro-Mobility

For drivers, navigating modern streets has become a high-stress endeavor. E-bikes, especially Class 3 models capable of reaching speeds up to 28 mph, often occupy a gray area between bicycles and motorcycles. Many riders operate them on sidewalks, blow through red lights, or weave unpredictably between lanes of slow-moving traffic without signaling.

When a collision occurs between a 4,000-pound SUV and a 50-pound e-bike, the physical outcome is almost always devastating for the rider. However, the legal and financial outcome for the driver can be equally ruinous, especially if there is no clear evidence of who was at fault. 

This new state-level reporting system aims to bridge that gap. By utilizing citizen-submitted data, traffic planners can identify hot spots for reckless riding, deploy targeted law enforcement, and even issue fines to registered commercial delivery riders. But to make this system work, drivers need reliable ways to document what happens on the tarmac.

## How to Report an Incident Safely

If you witness a reckless e-bike or e-scooter rider, safety should always remain your first priority. 

* **Do not engage:** Avoid confronting the rider or trying to block their path with your vehicle. Road rage incidents involving micro-mobility riders have escalated quickly in urban centers.
* **Let your dash cam do the work:** Do not try to film the rider with your handheld smartphone while driving. Instead, rely on your dash cam's loop recording or press the manual emergency save button on your camera unit to lock the footage.
* **Use the official portal:** Once you are safely parked, access your state's digital reporting portal or non-emergency traffic app to submit the video, along with the time, date, and location of the incident.

## Bottom Line / Our Verdict

The introduction of state-level reporting systems for reckless micro-mobility riders is a necessary evolution of our shared roadways. As electric bikes and scooters continue to grow in popularity throughout 2025, clear boundaries must be established to keep both motorists and pedestrians safe.

For drivers, investing in a high-quality dual- or triple-channel dash cam like the **Vantrue N4 Pro** is the smartest move you can make this year. It provides peace of mind, protects your insurance premiums, and ensures you have the evidence required to hold reckless road users accountable. For riders, riding defensively and using safety-focused gear like the **Garmin Varia RCT715** will ensure that these new reporting tools remain a benefit to the community rather than a threat to your daily commute.
