## Holiday Travel Just Got a Massive Electric Upgrade

Holiday travel is notorious for draining your patience and your wallet. Between clogged interstates and fluctuating gas prices, driving home for the holidays often feels like a chore. However, for electric vehicle drivers planning their cross-country treks this Thanksgiving, there is exceptional news on the horizon. IONNA, the charging joint venture backed by eight of the world's biggest automakers, has announced a sweeping 50% discount on all DC fast charging sessions across its rapidly expanding network for Thanksgiving weekend 2025.

Running from Wednesday before Thanksgiving through Cyber Monday, this half-price incentive is one of the most aggressive promotional moves we have seen in the public EV infrastructure space. As competition heats up between regional charging corridors and Tesla's long-dominant Supercharger network, IONNA's holiday discount is designed to turn heads, drive traffic to its flagship stations, and prove that road-tripping an electric car is not just viable, but considerably cheaper than driving a combustion vehicle.

## Who is IONNA and Why Does This Move Matter?

If the name IONNA still sounds unfamiliar, you have likely been watching its parent brands instead. Founded as a mega-coalition between BMW, General Motors, Honda, Hyundai, Kia, Mercedes-Benz, Stellantis, and Toyota, IONNA launched with a singular mission: build an accessible, ultra-reliable high-power charging network across North America to rival Tesla's Supercharger footprint. 

Targeting at least 30,000 high-power charge points deployed by 2030, IONNA began opening its first operational hubs earlier this year. Each site is engineered to deliver speeds up to 400 kW, equipped with both Combined Charging System (CCS) and North American Charging Standard (NACS) connectors, and powered entirely by renewable energy.

Slashing rates by half during the single busiest driving weekend of the year is a bold marketing power play. Historically, charging networks have kept rates static or even bumped peak pricing during holidays to manage demand. By cutting rates in half, IONNA is signaling confidence in its hardware uptime, canopy reliability, and software throughput.

## The Promotion Details: How to Save at the Plugs

The mechanics of the discount are straightforward. Drivers pulling into any operational IONNA hub between 12:01 AM on Wednesday, November 26, through 11:59 PM on Monday, December 1, 2025, will automatically receive a 50% reduction on per-kilowatt-hour (kWh) fees.

* **No exclusive brand locks:** While IONNA is backed by specific automakers, any EV capable of DC fast charging—including Teslas, Rivians, and Fords—can use the stations and claim the 50% discount.
* **App and Plug & Charge:** The discount applies automatically whether you initiate charging via the IONNA mobile app, credit card terminal on the pedestal, or supported automaker Plug & Charge profiles.
* **Idle fees still apply:** To keep queues moving smoothly, idle fees will remain in full effect if your car sits connected after reaching its target charge level.

Considering average public DC fast charging rates hover between $0.42 and $0.56 per kWh depending on state tariffs, this promotion effectively cuts the cost down to roughly $0.21 to $0.28 per kWh. That means completely replenishing a massive 80 kWh battery pack will run you around $18 to $22—delivering over 250 miles of highway driving for less than half the price of filling a standard 15-gallon gas tank.

## Top EVs Built to Dominate the IONNA Network

If you are planning to take advantage of IONNA’s lightning-fast 400 kW pedestals this Thanksgiving, your charging speed will largely depend on your vehicle's underlying architecture. Here are three benchmark EVs and an essential accessory that excel at these stations:

### 1. Hyundai Ioniq 5 (MSRP: ~$42,500)
Equipped with an advanced 800-volt charging architecture, the Hyundai Ioniq 5 remains an undisputed value-for-money road trip champion. At an IONNA ultra-fast stall, the Ioniq 5 can surge from 10% to 80% charge in just 18 minutes under optimal conditions. Paired with a spacious cabin and retro-futuristic styling, it is a quintessential long-haul cruiser.

### 2. Chevrolet Equinox EV (MSRP: ~$34,995)
For budget-conscious families looking for range without breaking the bank, GM's Chevy Equinox EV delivers over 315 miles of EPA-estimated range on select trims. While it relies on a standard 400-volt system topping out around 150 kW, its flat charging curve makes holiday pitstops predictable, painless, and thanks to IONNA's promo, remarkably inexpensive.

### 3. Mercedes-Benz EQE SUV (MSRP: ~$77,900)
For those wanting first-class comfort during holiday traffic jams, the Mercedes EQE SUV delivers exceptional sound isolation, active suspension, and seamless digital route planning. As a founding partner in IONNA, Mercedes vehicles benefit from flawless native Plug & Charge integration at these stations, initiating power within seconds of plugging in.

### 4. A2Z Typhoon Pro NACS to CCS1 Adapter (~$199)
If you drive an EV with a native CCS1 port and pull up to an IONNA station featuring dedicated NACS stalls, having an adapter like the A2Z Typhoon Pro in your trunk guarantees you never face an incompatible plug. Rated up to 500A and 1000V, it provides heavy-duty peace of mind on congested holiday corridors.

## Why This Deal Signals a Turning Point for EV Travel

For years, EV skeptics have pointed to non-Tesla public charging as an inconsistent web of broken screens, derated dispensers, and confusing pricing structures. IONNA’s aggressive Thanksgiving promotion highlights two major structural shifts underway in late 2025:

First, public charging is becoming an amenity-first customer experience. IONNA hubs are built with full weather canopies, bright overhead lighting, dedicated trash bins, security cameras, and proximity to convenience retail. By pairing premium site design with deep holiday discounts, the network is directly combating charge anxiety.

Second, price competition is finally emerging. Until recently, charging networks rarely competed on price because availability was too scarce. By offering half-price charging over the busiest driving stretch of the year, IONNA is establishing a precedent that could force rivals like Electrify America and EVgo to rethink their seasonal loyalty promotions.

## The Bottom Line / Our Verdict

IONNA’s 50% Thanksgiving discount is a massive win for EV drivers. It turns what is traditionally the most expensive and stressful weekend on American highways into a showcase of how affordable and modern electric travel can be. If your travel route crosses paths with an active IONNA station this holiday season, planning your rest stops around their ultra-fast stalls is an absolute no-brainer for both your schedule and your wallet.

## Деталі акції: як заощадити на зарядних станціях

Умови акції максимально прості. Водії, які заїдуть на будь-який працюючий хаб IONNA з 00:01 середи, 26 листопада, до 23:59 понеділка, 1 грудня 2025 року, автоматично отримають знижку 50% на вартість кожного кіловат-години (кВт·год).

* **Жодних обмежень за брендами:** Хоча IONNA заснована конкретними автовиробниками, будь-який електромобіль із підтримкою швидкої зарядки постійним струмом (включно з Tesla, Rivian і Ford) може заряджатися на цих станціях і отримати 50% знижку.
* **Застосунок і Plug & Charge:** Знижка застосовується автоматично незалежно від того, як ви запускаєте сесію: через мобільний застосунок IONNA, банківський термінал на колонці чи підтримуваний профіль Plug & Charge вашого авто.
* **Плата за простій залишається чинною:** Щоб уникнути штучних черг, плата за простій стягуватиметься в повному обсязі, якщо авто залишається підключеним після завершення заряджання до встановленого ліміту.

З огляду на те, що середні тарифи на швидку DC-зарядку в США коливаються від $0,42 до $0,56 за кВт·год залежно від штату, ця акція фактично знижує вартість приблизно до $0,21–$0,28 за кВт·год. Це означає, що повна зарядка великої батареї ємністю 80 кВт·год обійдеться лише в $18–$22, забезпечивши понад 400 км (250 миль) ходу трасою — менш ніж за половину вартості повного стандартного 15-галонного бака бензину.
