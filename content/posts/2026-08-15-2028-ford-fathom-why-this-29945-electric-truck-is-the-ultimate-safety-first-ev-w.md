## Next-Gen ADAS: Ford Co-Pilot360 Evolution

The marquee highlight of the 2028 Ford Fathom is the standard inclusion of an updated **Ford Co-Pilot360 Active Safety Suite**. While current budget compacts often gatekeep crucial safety sensors behind expensive trim packages, Ford is targeting zero-fatality roadway design across all trim levels.

### 1. 360-Degree Pre-Collision Assist with Evasive Steering
Equipped with upgraded high-definition solid-state radar and forward multi-spectrum cameras, the Fathom's Pre-Collision Assist can detect pedestrians, cyclists, and cross-traffic intersections even in poor weather or zero-light environments. Evasive Steering Assist activates seamlessly if braking distance alone is insufficient to prevent an impact.

### 2. Trailer-Aware Blind Spot Information System (BLIS)
Compact trucks frequently suffer from poor rearward visibility when hauling cargo beds or towing recreational gear. Ford’s BLIS system on the Fathom uses millimeter-wave radar embedded directly into the taillights, extending blind-spot coverage down the full length of hitched trailers without requiring external wired sensors.

### 3. Lane-Centering and BlueCruise Ready Hardware
Even on the baseline $29,945 trim, the Fathom includes intelligent Lane-Centering with Road Edge Detection. Capable of interpreting gravel lines, curbs, and faded lane paint, the system minimizes accidental lane drift. Higher trims will support hands-free highway driving via BlueCruise hardware already pre-integrated into the chassis.

## Structural Engineering and Battery Cell Enclosure Safety

Crash safety in electric pickup trucks presents unique challenges. Low-slung structural battery packs add significant weight, which increases kinetic energy during high-speed collisions. Ford has engineered the Fathom's chassis from high-strength boron steel and hydroformed aluminum crash rails designed to channel impact energy around the occupant survival cell.

Key passive safety features include:
* **Isolated Extruded Aluminum Battery Armor:** Prevents thermal runaway and cell puncture during severe side-pole impacts (targeting the rigorous IIHS Side 2.0 test protocols).
* **Front-Trunk (Frunk) Progressive Crumple Zone:** Because there is no internal combustion engine block under the hood, engineers designed multi-stage crumple zones that absorb front-impact forces far more progressively than traditional gas trucks.
* **Smart Airbag Deployment Matrix:** Includes standard front-center airbags to prevent occupant collision during side impacts, alongside dual-stage knee, curtain, and thorax protection.
