## Introduction

Yacht Club Games has captured our hearts once again with the release of *Mina the Hollower*, a gothic, Game Boy Color-inspired action-adventure that feels both wonderfully nostalgic and punishingly modern. As players guide the whip-wielding mouse Mina through the decaying landscape of Tenebrous Isle, they are faced with tough combat, intricate level design, and surprisingly deep narrative choices. 

One of the most agonizing decisions you will encounter in the mid-game is whether or not to seal the Duke of the Sunken Parish inside his ancestral tomb. This choice isn't just a simple moral fork in the road; it fundamentally alters your rewards, changes world-state elements, and impacts your endgame experience. In this comprehensive guide, we break down both paths so you can make the absolute best decision for your playthrough.

---

## Who is the Duke of the Sunken Parish?

Before making your choice, it helps to understand who you are dealing with. The Duke is a tragic figure who attempted to harness the Spark—the mysterious energy source keeping Tenebrous Isle alive—to save his dying lineage. Instead, the raw power mutated him into a towering, semi-spectral monstrosity. 

When you finally track him down to the weeping crypts beneath the Sunken Parish, he is temporarily subdued. A holy relic, the Seal of the Spark, lies nearby. You are given a prompt to either place the seal on his sarcophagus, locking him away forever, or smash the seal, allowing his spirit to linger in the mortal realm. 

---

## Option A: Sealing the Duke in the Tomb

If you choose to place the Seal of the Spark on the sarcophagus, you choose the path of containment and safety. This is generally considered the "righteous" or conservative path.

### The Consequences:
* **The Parish Calms Down:** Once the Duke is sealed, the aggressive spectral enemies in the Sunken Parish area decrease in number. This makes backtracking through the zone significantly easier.
* **The Questline Ends:** Sealing the Duke immediately concludes his personal questline. You will not see him again for the rest of the game.

### The Rewards:
* **The Hollower's Peace Charm:** Upon sealing the tomb, Mina receives this unique passive charm. It grants a 15% reduction to all shadow and elemental damage taken. It is an incredibly powerful defensive tool, especially for players struggling with late-game boss fights.
* **Safe Passage Achievement:** You unlock a unique achievement/trophy for resolving the parish crisis peacefully.

---

## Option B: Leaving the Tomb Open (Breaking the Seal)

If you decide to smash the seal or simply walk away, you choose the path of chaos, curiosity, and high risk. This is the path preferred by completionists and players looking for a mechanical challenge.

### The Consequences:
* **Unleashed Terror:** The Duke escapes his physical vessel. The Sunken Parish becomes shrouded in a thick, purple fog, and high-level phantom enemies begin patrolling the area.
* **The Roaming Boss Fight:** The Duke's wraith will randomly invade other zones in the game, acting as a terrifying roaming mini-boss. He can ambush you when you least expect it, turning routine exploration into a fight for your life.

### The Rewards:
* **The Duke's Crest (Weapon Upgrade):** Defeating the Duke's roaming wraith in his final encounter rewards you with the Duke's Crest. This item allows you to upgrade your primary whip or sidearms to their maximum tier, unlocking devastating secondary attacks.
* **Access to the Secret Dungeon:** Keeping the Duke free is the only way to unlock the "Sunken Treasury," a high-difficulty bonus dungeon containing some of the best lore drops and currency in the game.

## Bottom Line / Our Verdict

So, should you seal the Duke in the tomb?

For **casual players and first-timers**, we highly recommend **Sealing the Duke**. The *Hollower's Peace* charm provides a fantastic safety net for the brutal late-game areas, and keeping the Sunken Parish free of high-level phantoms will save you a massive amount of frustration.

However, if you are a **hardcore completionist or a veteran of Yacht Club Games**, you should absolutely **Leave the Tomb Open**. While the roaming boss fights are incredibly tough, the *Duke's Crest* weapon upgrade and access to the *Sunken Treasury* secret dungeon are simply too good to pass up. It offers the most satisfying narrative resolution and tests your skills to their absolute limit. 

Whichever path you choose, make sure your reflexes are sharp and your controller is ready—Tenebrous Isle does not forgive mistakes easily!
