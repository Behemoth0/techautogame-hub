## PC Gaming Royalty Returns to Print in 2025

In an era dominated by digital feeds and ephemeral social media posts, there is still something profoundly satisfying about flipping through a physical gaming magazine. PC Gamer has long stood as the gold standard of computer gaming journalism, and their latest issue for 2025 is a absolute feast for physical media enthusiasts and hardcore PC gamers alike. 

Available on newsstands and digital storefronts starting today, the new issue lands with a massive splash. It features an exclusive deep-dive cover story on Capcom’s newly resurrected samurai action franchise, *Onimusha: Way of the Sword*. But that is far from all. Tucked inside this issue, readers will find a physical collector's dream: a gigantic double-sided A1 poster and an exclusive bonus mini-magazine showcasing the updated Top 100 PC Games list. Whether you are looking for your next obsession or simply want to deck out your gaming cave, this edition is essential reading.

## Onimusha: Way of the Sword – The Demon Slayer Reborn

For years, action-game connoisseurs have been begging Capcom to reach into its legendary vaults and bring back *Onimusha*. With *Onimusha: Way of the Sword*, those prayers have finally been answered. PC Gamer’s world-exclusive cover story dives deep behind closed doors at Capcom’s Osaka studio to reveal how this beloved classic is being reimagined for modern PC hardware.

Inside the feature, the team explores the game's shift toward high-speed, precision-focused swordplay powered by the latest iteration of the RE Engine. The developers detail how they are blending classic soul-absorbing mechanics with modern parry-heavy combat systems made popular by modern action titles. PC Gamer delivers hands-on impressions, detailing blistering frame rates, ultra-wide monitor support, and ray-traced shadow effects that make feudal Japan look stunningly atmospheric. For fans of samurai mythology, supernatural demon hunting, and precise melee combat, this feature alone makes grabbing the issue worthwhile.

## Double Bonus: Huge A1 Poster and the Top 100 Mini-Mag

Capcom’s demon-slaying epic is only half the draw. Print physical releases live and die by their bonus extras, and PC Gamer has pulled out all the stops for this issue.

First, subscribers and newsstand buyers get a massive, high-quality A1 double-sided poster. One side features breathtaking key art from *Onimusha: Way of the Sword*, showcasing the protagonist locked in combat against a terrifying demonic general. The reverse side celebrates legendary PC gaming heritage with custom artwork dedicated to 2025’s top PC titles—perfect for framing in any gaming setup.

Second, the issue comes bundled with a standalone 32-page "Top 100 PC Games" mini-magazine. Re-evaluating the greatest games playable on PC today, this definitive mini-mag incorporates the massive RPG releases, indie darlings, and multiplayer shooters of the past year. It serves as an incredible buying guide for anyone looking to build out their Steam wishlist with tested, high-quality experiences.
