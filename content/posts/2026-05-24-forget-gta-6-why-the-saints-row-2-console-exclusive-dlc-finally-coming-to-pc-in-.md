## The Tragedy of the Saints Row 2 PC Port

To understand why this is such a massive deal, we have to look back at 2009. When *Saints Row 2* made its way to PC, it was handled by an external studio that delivered a port so unstable it practically ran on thoughts and prayers. It suffered from speed-up bugs tied to CPU clock speeds, frequent crashes, and terrible controller support. 

Worst of all, PC players never received the two major story DLCs. *Ultor Exposed* brought back Tera Patrick and added crucial missions exposing the sinister corporate overlords of Stilwater. *Corporate Warfare* focused on a brutal civil war within the Ultor Corporation, delivering some of the most action-packed setpieces in the franchise. For over a decade, these were trapped on aging Xbox 360 and PlayStation 3 hardware.

Now, in 2025, the emulation scene has matured to the point where running the Xbox 360 version of *Saints Row 2* on PC—complete with both DLCs, upscaled resolutions, and 60+ FPS performance—is not just possible; it is the definitive way to play the game.
