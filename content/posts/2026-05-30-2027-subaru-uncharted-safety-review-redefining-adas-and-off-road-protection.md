## Introduction: The New Frontier of Rugged Safety

Subaru has long been synonymous with outdoor adventure and standard-setting safety. With the unveiling of the highly anticipated **2027 Subaru Uncharted**, the Japanese automaker is taking its legendary reputation into entirely new territory. Designed as a rugged, mid-size adventure crossover that bridges the gap between family commuter and hardcore overlander, the Uncharted isn't just built to conquer trails—it is engineered to protect its occupants like nothing else in its class.

As safety standards have grown exponentially more stringent since the landmark safety updates of 2025, Subaru has stepped up to the plate. The 2027 Uncharted introduces a host of revolutionary active driver-assistance systems (ADAS), structural enhancements, and off-road safety innovations. In this comprehensive safety review, we will dissect how the Uncharted performs under scrutiny, what advanced tech it brings to the table, and the essential safety gear you should pair with it.

## Next-Gen EyeSight X: The Brains Behind the Adventure

For over a decade, Subaru’s EyeSight driver assist technology has been a benchmark for camera-based safety. The 2027 Subaru Uncharted debuts **EyeSight X**, a highly advanced iteration that integrates solid-state LiDAR sensors alongside the traditional dual-camera setup. This hybrid sensor fusion marks a massive leap forward from the systems seen in 2025 models.

By combining optical cameras with LiDAR, the Uncharted can map its surroundings in three dimensions, even in heavy rain, dense fog, or pitch-black trail conditions. Key features of the EyeSight X suite include:

*   **Automated Emergency Steering (AES):** Operating at speeds up to 50 mph, this system can actively steer the vehicle around obstacles within the lane if braking alone isn't enough to avoid a collision.
*   **Intersection Collision Avoidance:** Utilizing wide-angle radar sensors, the Uncharted detects cross-traffic, cyclists, and pedestrians at blind intersections, applying the brakes automatically if a collision is imminent.
*   **Level 2+ Hands-Free Highway Assist:** On pre-mapped highways, the Uncharted allows for hands-free driving up to 75 mph, utilizing a driver-monitoring infrared camera to ensure the operator's eyes remain firmly on the road.

## Structural Integrity: Building on 2025 IIHS Standards

The foundation of the Uncharted’s safety is the Subaru Global Platform II (SGP2). Following the Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS) update to its side-impact testing protocols back in 2025—which introduced a heavier barrier traveling at higher speeds to simulate modern electric SUVs—Subaru reinforced the B-pillars and door beams of the Uncharted with ultra-high-strength hot-stamped steel.

Internal crash-testing projections indicate that the 2027 Subaru Uncharted is on track for a sweep of "Good" ratings in all IIHS crashworthiness categories, comfortably securing the coveted **Top Safety Pick+** award. Additionally, the cabin features a unique multi-chamber airbag system, including a front-center airbag designed to prevent front-seat occupants from colliding with one another during a severe side impact.

## Off-Road Active Safety: Smart Terrain Management

Unlike traditional crossovers that disable their safety systems the moment they leave the pavement, the 2027 Subaru Uncharted introduces **Predictive X-Mode**. This system uses the vehicle's forward-looking LiDAR to scan the topography of the trail up to 50 feet ahead.

If the system detects a sudden drop-off, deep ruts, or slippery mud, it pre-charges the braking system, adjusts the torque distribution of the Symmetrical All-Wheel Drive system, and optimizes the Hill Descent Control. This proactive approach prevents loss of traction before it even occurs, keeping you in control on treacherous mountain passes.

## Top Safety Gear Upgrades for Your Uncharted

While the 2027 Subaru Uncharted comes packed with factory safety tech, serious adventurers should always supplement their vehicle with high-quality aftermarket safety gear. Here are our top product recommendations to keep in your Uncharted:

### 1. Vantrue N4 Pro 3-Channel Dash Cam (~$299.99)
An absolute must-have for documenting highway incidents and off-road trail rides. The Vantrue N4 Pro features a Sony STARVIS 2 sensor for incredible night vision, recording the front, cabin, and rear of your vehicle simultaneously. It acts as an invaluable insurance policy and a backup set of eyes.

### 2. NOCO Boost Plus GB40 UltraSafe Lithium Jump Starter (~$99.95)
If you are venturing far off the grid, a dead battery can quickly turn into a survival situation. The NOCO GB40 is a compact, spark-proof lithium jump starter capable of delivering 1,000 amps. It can jump-start the Uncharted's engine up to 20 times on a single charge and doubles as a power bank for your devices.

### 3. Viair 88P Portable Air Compressor (~$89.95)
Proper tire pressure is vital for safety. When transitioning from highway driving to rugged trails, you will need to "air down" your tires for traction. The Viair 88P is a heavy-duty portable compressor that connects directly to your car battery, allowing you to quickly and safely inflate your tires back to highway pressures before heading home.

### 4. Autel MaxiLink ML629 OBD2 Scanner (~$119.00)
Modern ADAS systems rely heavily on complex electronic sensors. If a warning light pops up on your dashboard while you are miles from civilization, the Autel ML629 allows you to read and clear ABS, SRS (airbag), engine, and transmission codes instantly, helping you diagnose whether a fault is minor or requires immediate attention.

## The Bottom Line: Our Verdict

With the 2027 Uncharted, Subaru has successfully proven that rugged, off-road capability does not require compromising on passenger safety. By integrating cutting-edge solid-state LiDAR into its EyeSight X suite and reinforcing the chassis to meet and exceed modern crash standards, Subaru has built one of the safest adventure vehicles ever conceived. Whether you are navigating a treacherous winter blizzard or crawling over rocky terrain, the Uncharted provides an unparalleled safety net. For families and adventurers alike, it is a masterclass in modern automotive engineering.
