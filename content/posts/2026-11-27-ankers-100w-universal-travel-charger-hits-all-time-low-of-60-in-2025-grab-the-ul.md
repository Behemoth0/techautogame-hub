## Why GaN Tech Matters: Shrinking the Power Brick

To appreciate why Anker's 100W charger is so celebrated, you have to look under the hood. Traditional power adapters rely on silicon-based transistors. Silicon has served the tech world admirably for decades, but it generates substantial heat when converting high wattages, necessitating larger enclosures and built-in heat sinks to prevent thermal runaway.

Enter Gallium Nitride (GaN). GaN components transfer electricity with significantly higher efficiency than silicon, generating a fraction of the waste heat. Because it stays cooler, internal parts can sit much closer together. The result? A 100-watt wall adapter that matches or beats the output of Apple's standard MacBook Pro bricks while sitting at roughly half the footprint.

Anker’s proprietary GaNPrime technology pairs this material advantage with ActiveShield 2.0 temperature monitoring, which checks unit temperatures millions of times a day to dynamically throttle power if things get uncomfortably warm inside tight hotel sockets.

## What Makes the Anker 100W Charger Stand Out?

Packing 100W of total output into a pocket-sized package with folding prongs is an accomplishment on its own, but the real utility lies in port selection and power distribution:

- **Dual USB-C and Single USB-A Setup:** You get two high-speed USB-C ports alongside a legacy USB-A port for older devices, e-readers, or smartwatches.
- **Dynamic Power Distribution (PowerIQ 4.0):** If you plug in a power-hungry 16-inch MacBook Pro by itself, the primary USB-C port cranks out a full 100W, taking your laptop from 0% to 50% in roughly 30 minutes. If you plug in a smartphone alongside it, the charger instantly negotiates, splitting the power (e.g., 65W for your laptop and 30W for your phone) without interrupting the connection.
- **Universal Voltage Compatibility:** Handling 100V–240V inputs effortlessly, it only requires a basic plug prong adapter to work anywhere from Tokyo to London.
- **Travel-Friendly Form Factor:** The fold-down prongs mean you can toss it into a sling bag without worrying about scratching your tablet screen or tearing cable jackets.
