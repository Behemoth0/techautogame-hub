## How to Choose the Right Gift for a PC Gamer

Before pulling the trigger, take a quick peek at their current setup or ask a few subtle questions:

1. **Check Form Factors:** If buying a keyboard, observe their desk space. Full-sized boards with number pads take up huge real estate; compact layouts like 75% or 65% leave far more room for mouse swiping.
2. **Look for Swappability:** Peripherals that use standard USB-C connections or hot-swappable switches last considerably longer because cables and individual switch parts can be easily replaced if worn down.
3. **Avoid Generic "Gaming Bundles":** Steer clear of unbranded 4-in-1 combo boxes found on big-box shelves. While tempting at $30, the plastic sensors, membranes, and tinny audio will only disappoint. Dedicated picks like those from Logitech, Razer, Crucial, and Aula yield genuine longevity.
