## The King of Desk Setups Just Got More Affordable

If you have spent any time researching desk setups, productivity hacks, or ergonomic office gear over the last few years, you have undoubtedly run into the Logitech MX Master series. It has long been hailed as the undisputed king of office mice. However, quality comes at a price, and the flagship Logitech MX Master 3S has stubbornly held onto its premium $100+ price tag since its launch.

That changes today. In a surprising move that will delight remote workers, programmers, and creative professionals alike, the Logitech MX Master 3S has dropped below the $100 threshold, currently retailing around $93 to $96 at major retailers. If you have been waiting for the perfect moment to upgrade your workspace in 2025, this is officially your green light.

Let’s dive into why this price drop is such a big deal, how the mouse holds up in 2025, and how it compares to its closest competitors.

## Why the MX Master 3S Remains the Gold Standard

It is easy to dismiss a mouse as just another pointer tool, but the MX Master 3S is a highly specialized instrument designed to reduce wrist strain and speed up your workflow. Here are the standout features that justify its reputation:

### 1. The MagSpeed Electromagnetic Scroll Wheel
Once you experience Logitech’s MagSpeed wheel, it is incredibly difficult to go back to a standard mechanical scroll wheel. Built from machined steel, it can scroll up to 1,000 lines in a single second when spun quickly, automatically shifting from a tactile ratcheting mode to a silent, free-spinning hyper-fast mode. If you work with massive Excel spreadsheets, long lines of code, or endless PDFs, this wheel is a life-changer.

### 2. Whisper-Quiet Clicks
One of the biggest upgrades the "S" variant brought over the older MX Master 3 was the introduction of Quiet Click switches. They offer the same tactile feedback as before but reduce click noise by 90%. This makes it the perfect companion for quiet office environments, coffee shops, or late-night work sessions at home where you don't want to wake your family.

### 3. Track-on-Glass 8K DPI Sensor
Most optical mice struggle on shiny surfaces, especially glass. The MX Master 3S features an 8,000 DPI optical sensor that tracks flawlessly on almost any surface, including dark glass. Whether you are working at a minimalist glass desk or a wooden kitchen table, you won't need a mousepad.

### 4. Unrivaled Ergonomics and Customization
The mouse is sculpted to support your hand and wrist in a natural position. It also features a thumb rest with an integrated gesture button and a secondary thumb wheel for horizontal scrolling (perfect for video editing timelines). Through the Logi Options+ software, every single button can be customized with application-specific profiles.

## Is the MX Master 3S Worth It in 2025?

Absolutely. Even as we head deeper into 2025, the MX Master 3S does not feel dated. It charges via USB-C, offering up to 70 days of battery life on a single full charge (and three hours of use from a mere one-minute quick charge). 

Furthermore, the multi-device connectivity is seamless. With Logitech Flow, you can move your cursor across up to three different computers (even switching between Windows and macOS) and copy-paste text, images, and files between them effortlessly.

If there is one critique, it is that the mouse is strictly designed for right-handed users. Left-handed productivity enthusiasts are unfortunately left out of the loop here, and gamers might find the 141-gram weight a bit too heavy for fast-paced competitive shooters. But for pure productivity, design, and office tasks, it has no equal.
