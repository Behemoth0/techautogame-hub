## Introduction

Cozy gaming has evolved from a niche subgenre into a massive cultural phenomenon. Among the giants of this space, few titles hold as special and quirky a place in our hearts as *Hidden Folks*. Originally released in 2017 by indie developers Adriaan de Jongh and Sylvain Tegroeg, the game charmed millions with its intricate, hand-drawn interactive landscapes, monochrome aesthetic, and incredibly endearing mouth-made sound effects. 

Now, the wait is almost over. The developers have officially announced that the highly anticipated sequel, *Hidden Folks 2*, is slated for a release in 2025. If you spent hours squinting at your screen trying to find a tiny snake in a bustling jungle, you know how addictive this formula is. But with larger maps, more complex interactions, and even deeper layers of detail promised for the sequel, your old gaming setup might need an upgrade. 

To help you get ready for the ultimate hide-and-seek experience, we’ve rounded up the absolute best tech gadgets, tablets, and accessories to ensure your eyes and fingers are fully prepared for *Hidden Folks 2* in 2025.

## What We Know About Hidden Folks 2 So Far

*Hidden Folks* succeeded because of its simplicity and artisanal charm. Every single asset in the game was hand-drawn on paper, scanned, and meticulously animated. The sequel promises to double down on this formula while introducing some highly requested quality-of-life features.

We can expect massive new maps spanning multiple historical eras, fantasy realms, and modern urban environments. The developer team has hinted at improved hint systems, more interactive environments where your clicks cause chain reactions, and potentially new color themes (like sepia or high-contrast modes) to make the game more accessible to players with visual impairments. 

Because the game relies on spotting tiny, pixel-thin drawings hidden amidst hundreds of moving parts, the device you play on makes a massive difference. Playing on a sub-par screen can lead to eye strain, while clumsy touch controls can result in frustrating misclicks. 

## Top Tech Gear to Experience Hidden Folks 2 in 2025

To get the most out of *Hidden Folks 2*, you need a device with an ultra-sharp display, excellent contrast, and precise input methods. Whether you prefer playing on the couch, on your daily commute, or at your desk, here is the best gear to buy right now.

### 1. The Ultimate Portable Console: Steam Deck OLED
* **Approximate Price:** $549 (512GB Model)

If you want to play the PC version of *Hidden Folks 2* with maximum comfort, the Steam Deck OLED is the gold standard. The original game ran beautifully on the standard Steam Deck, but the sequel deserves the gorgeous OLED upgrade. 

The 7.4-inch 90Hz OLED display offers an infinite contrast ratio. In a game like *Hidden Folks*, where you are looking at black lines on a white or sepia background, the deep blacks and vibrant whites of an OLED screen make the hand-drawn art pop like never before. Furthermore, the Steam Deck’s dual trackpads are perfect for simulating mouse precision, allowing you to pan across massive maps and click on tiny targets with ease.

### 2. The Best Premium iOS Option: Apple iPad Air 11-inch (M2, 2024)
* **Approximate Price:** $599

*Hidden Folks* is arguably best enjoyed as a touch-screen game. Curling up on the couch with an iPad is the quintessential cozy gaming experience. The latest iPad Air powered by the M2 chip offers a stunning Liquid Retina display with a resolution of 2360 x 1640. 

This high pixel density ensures that even the smallest hand-drawn characters remain razor-sharp. The M2 chip is absolute overkill for a 2D puzzle game, meaning you will get buttery-smooth scrolling and panning across the game's largest maps without a single frame drop. Plus, the battery life is excellent, easily lasting through marathon searching sessions.

### 3. The Precision Tool: Apple Pencil Pro
* **Approximate Price:** $129

If you choose to play *Hidden Folks 2* on an iPad, using your bare fingers can sometimes block your view of the tiny details you are trying to find. That is where the Apple Pencil Pro comes in. 

Using a stylus transforms the game into an interactive coloring book experience. The Apple Pencil Pro offers pixel-perfect precision, allowing you to tap on a single blade of grass to see if a bug is hiding behind it. The new haptic feedback engine in the Pencil Pro can also make clicking interactive objects feel incredibly tactile and satisfying.

### 4. The Android Alternative: Samsung Galaxy Tab S9 FE
* **Approximate Price:** $449

For Android enthusiasts, the Samsung Galaxy Tab S9 FE is the perfect middle-ground device. It features a bright, sharp 10.9-inch 90Hz display that makes navigating hand-drawn worlds an absolute breeze. 

Unlike Apple, Samsung generously includes the S Pen in the box, saving you from having to purchase a stylus separately. The S Pen is lightweight, incredibly responsive, and makes tapping through *Hidden Folks 2*'s busy environments comfortable and ergonomic, reducing wrist fatigue during long play sessions.

### 5. The Desk Setup Companion: Logitech Pebble Mouse 2 M350S
* **Approximate Price:** $29

If you prefer playing cozy games on a laptop or desktop computer, you don't want a heavy, aggressive-looking gaming mouse. The Logitech Pebble Mouse 2 is a sleek, minimalist, and ultra-quiet mouse that fits perfectly into any cozy aesthetic.

Its silent clicking technology is particularly important here. Since *Hidden Folks 2* requires hundreds of clicks as you interact with doors, trees, and characters, a loud, clicky mouse can quickly become annoying. The Pebble Mouse 2 keeps things whisper-quiet, allowing you to focus entirely on the game’s delightful mouth-made sound effects.

## Cozy Gaming Tips for Hidden Folks 2

To make sure you are fully prepared for the launch in 2025, consider these setup tips:
* **Invest in a Matte Screen Protector:** If you play on an iPad or tablet, a matte (or paper-like) screen protector reduces glare from overhead lights and gives the screen a textured feel that complements the hand-drawn aesthetic of the game.
* **Use Night Mode:** *Hidden Folks* features a built-in night mode (white lines on a black background). If you plan to play in bed before sleeping, make sure to toggle this on to reduce blue light exposure.
* **Wear Headphones:** The game's sound design is half the fun. Every single sound effect is voiced by the developers. Wearing a comfortable pair of wireless earbuds will help you fully immerse yourself in the whimsical world.

## Bottom Line / Our Verdict

*Hidden Folks 2* is shaping up to be one of the most delightful indie releases of 2025. Its hand-drawn art style demands a high-quality screen, and its interactive gameplay rewards precision. 

If you want the absolute best experience, we highly recommend pairing the **Apple iPad Air M2** with the **Apple Pencil Pro**. The combination of a gorgeous Liquid Retina display and stylus precision makes it the ultimate way to enjoy this interactive masterpiece. For PC gamers, the **Steam Deck OLED** offers an unmatched portable experience that makes the monochrome art look sharper than ever. Whichever device you choose, prepare your eyes—the search begins next year!
