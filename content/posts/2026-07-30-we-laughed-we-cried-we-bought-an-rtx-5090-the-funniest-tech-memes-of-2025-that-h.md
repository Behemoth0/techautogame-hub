## Introduction: Welcome to 2025, Where Your GPU Costs More Than a Used Honda Civic

Grab your favorite overpriced energy drink, sit back, and enjoy the hilarious, painfully accurate tech memes of 2025 that hit a little too close to home.

## 3. Slapping "Neural Quantum AI" on Literally Everything

If a product released in 2025 didn't have "AI" stuffed into its product title, did it even really exist? Tech companies spent the entire year frantically shoehorning artificial intelligence into places where nobody asked for it. ## 4. The Infinite Steam Backlog vs. Steam Summer Sale

Gabe Newell strikes again. The classic meme format of the guy looking back at another girl made its triumphant 2025 return, except this time it was gamers ignoring 600 unplayed, uninstalled titles in their library to buy a $4 indie farming simulator because it was 90% off.

Here are 3 reasons you bought another bundle despite having 400 unplayed games:

## Bottom Line

Look, 2025 has been a wild, expensive, and absurd year for technology and gaming. Our hardware is overheating, our appliances talk back to us, and our wallets are screaming for mercy. Real talk: If your power supply hasn't exploded yet, count your blessings, turn off "AI Turbo Boost," and actually go play one of those 500 games rotting in your Steam backlog today.
