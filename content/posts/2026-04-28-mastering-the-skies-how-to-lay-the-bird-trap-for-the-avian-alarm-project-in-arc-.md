## Introduction: The High Stakes of Arc Raiders in 2025

Since its highly anticipated launch, Arc Raiders has redefined the extraction shooter genre with its stunning visuals and the relentless threat of the ARC machines. As we move into 2025, the meta has shifted toward tactical utility and base-building within the Burrows. One of the most critical early-to-mid-game milestones for any Raider is the Avian Alarm project. This project isn't just a cosmetic upgrade for your hideout; it provides a vital early-warning system against machine incursions, giving you those precious extra seconds to prepare your defenses or hide your loot.

However, the most difficult part of this project is successfully laying the bird trap. Unlike standard machine scavenging, the Avian Alarm requires you to interact with the wasteland's remaining organic life—specifically, the hardy desert birds that have survived the machine apocalypse. In this guide, we will walk you through the precise steps to lay the trap, the best locations to ensure a catch, and the gaming gear you need to stay ahead of the ARC.

## Unlocking the Avian Alarm Project

Before you can start laying traps, you need to visit the Burrows and speak with the Quartermaster. The Avian Alarm project becomes available once you reach Reputation Level 4 with the 'Scavenger’s Guild.' You will need a blueprint that requires a baseline of materials: 10 Scrap Metal, 4 Copper Wires, and 2 Primitive Sensors. Once the project is active in your log, the game will grant you access to the 'Improvised Bird Trap' item in your crafting menu. 

It is important to note that the trap is a one-time-use item per deployment. If you fail the capture or if a machine destroys the trap, you will have to craft another one. Therefore, efficiency is key to avoiding wasted resources.

## How to Lay the Bird Trap: Step-by-Step

Laying the trap is more about stealth and positioning than raw firepower. Follow these steps to ensure your Avian Alarm project stays on track:

### 1. Identify the 'Avian Zones'
Birds in Arc Raiders don't just spawn anywhere. You need to look for areas with high verticality and minimal machine patrol paths. The 'Rust Canyons' and the 'Overgrown Observatory' are the two highest-yield zones for bird activity. Look for white markings on the rocks—a classic sign of nesting grounds.

### 2. Clearing the Immediate Perimeter
Before you place the trap, you must clear out any nearby 'Drones' or 'Small Walkers.' Machines are attracted to the mechanical clicking of the trap's mechanism. Use a suppressed weapon to take out enemies quietly. If you trigger an alarm, the birds will scatter, and you’ll have to wait 10 real-time minutes for them to return.

### 3. Placing the Bait
The trap requires 'Organic Grains,' which can be found in the abandoned silos near the Southern Farmstead. Place the trap on a flat surface, preferably on a ledge overlooking a valley. Interact with the trap to 'Prime' it. You will see a small UI indicator showing the 'Visibility' of your trap. Ensure it is not obscured by heavy brush, or the birds won't see the bait.

### 4. The Waiting Game
Once the trap is laid, you cannot stay near it. Move at least 30 meters away and find a vantage point. This is where a high-quality gaming monitor and headset become essential, as you need to listen for the specific 'chirp' that indicates a successful capture. If you see an ARC patrol approaching the trap, you must decide whether to defend it and risk a fight or let the trap be destroyed.

## Tactical Tips for Success

*   **Time of Day Matters:** Birds are most active during the 'Dawn' and 'Dusk' cycles in the game. Attempting this at night is significantly harder and increases the risk of running into 'Nighthawk' machines.
*   **Use Decoys:** If you are playing in a squad, have one player drop a 'Sound Decoy' 100 meters away from the trap. This will pull any roaming machines away from your objective.
*   **Extraction Priority:** Once you have captured the bird, it becomes a physical item in your inventory. If you die before extracting, you lose the progress. Head straight to the nearest extraction point—do not get greedy with extra loot.
