## GTA VI Waiting Room: Year 12 of Mass Psychosis

We entered 2025 with one promise echoing in our hearts: Grand Theft Auto VI is coming. But as the months tick by, the waiting has turned into an existential meme genre of its own. Gamers have reached a state of transcendental paranoia where any cloud formation shown in a Rockstar tweet is decoded as a secret release date.

## Bottom Line

Tech in 2025 is delightfully ridiculous. Companies want us to buy dedicated AI hardware to summarize emails we won't read, pay four figures for hardware that demands nuclear fusion, and subscribe to services to unlock heated seats in our gaming chairs. 

Here is the real, unvarnished advice behind the jokes: Don't let FOMO milk your bank account dry. Your RTX 3060 is doing just fine. Your base PS5 can still render pixels with dignity. And no, you do not need an NPU to enjoy indie platformers. Step away from the upgrade cart, ignore the Reddit hype-cycle, and enjoy the games you already own. Unless GTA VI actually drops next week—in which case, abandon all financial responsibility and take the week off.
