## The Dawn of a Reimagined Prancing Horse

When Ferrari first unveiled the SF90 Stradale, it sent shockwaves through the hypercar universe. It was the first series-production plug-in hybrid vehicle (PHEV) from Maranello, blending a twin-turbocharged V8 with three electric motors to shatter performance boundaries. Fast-forward to 2025, and Ferrari has taken this hyper-hybrid platform to its absolute zenith. The reinvented Ferrari SF90 lineage—led by track-bred innovations and enhanced road-going technology—redefines what automotive enthusiasts should expect from the next generation of performance machines.

Automakers worldwide are grappling with the transition between pure internal combustion and full electrification. Ferrari, however, refuses to treat hybridization as a compromise. Instead, the updated SF90 platform uses electric motors not just for emissions compliance, but as precise torque-vectoring weapons that elevate vehicle dynamics into science-fiction territory.

## Electrified V8 Powertrain: Pushing Beyond 1,000 Horsepower

At the mechanical heart of this reimagined beast sits an evolved 4.0-liter twin-turbocharged 90-degree V8 engine, paired with a trio of high-output axial-flux electric motors. In its latest setup, the combined system output eclipses an astonishing 1,016 horsepower, channeling pure adrenaline directly to all four wheels through an ultra-fast eight-speed dual-clutch transmission.

What makes this reinvented powertrain truly special is the instant low-end torque delivery. Two electric motors power the front axle independently, while a third motor—derived directly from Ferrari's Formula 1 RAC-e (Rotational Axis Control Electric) technology—sits between the engine and the gearbox. The result is seamless throttle response that completely eliminates turbo lag, propelling the SF90 from 0 to 60 mph in a blistering 2.1 seconds.

Furthermore, the updated 7.9 kWh lithium-ion battery pack provides a stealthy, all-electric eDrive range of roughly 15 miles. While short, it allows owners to quietly slip out of suburban neighborhoods before unleashing the twin-turbo fury on open asphalt.

## Active Aerodynamics and AI-Driven Dynamics

Aerodynamics in modern supercars can no longer be passive sheets of sculpted carbon fiber. The reinvented SF90 relies on active aero solutions that adapt millisecond-by-millisecond to road contours and driver inputs.

Key aerodynamic highlights include:

* **The Shut-Off Gurney:** A patented active rear wing system that drops down to minimize drag during high-speed straights, then immediately raises to generate massive downforce during heavy braking and sharp cornering.
* **Underbody Vortex Generators:** Redesigned strakes underneath the chassis that funnel air efficiently, generating ground-effect suction without disrupting high-speed stability.
* **eSSC 2.0 (electronic Side Slip Control):** Advanced AI algorithms that constantly compute grip levels at each individual tire, calculating exact torque distribution between the front electric motors and rear mechanical axle.

Whether attacking the high-speed curves of Fiorano or navigating tight mountain hairpins, the car feels hyper-responsive and telepathic, delivering confidence that makes even novice drivers feel like seasoned racers.

## Cockpit Tech: Digital Cockpit Meets Pure Motorsport DNA

Step inside the updated cockpit, and Ferrari's "eyes on the road, hands on the wheel" philosophy is immediately apparent. The cabin blends minimalist motorsport architecture with state-of-the-art consumer electronics.

A massive 16-inch curved digital instrument cluster dominates the driver's view, fully configurable to display navigation, performance telemetry, or hybrid power flows. Complementing the display is an upgraded head-up display (HUD) that projects shifting lights and critical driving data directly onto the windscreen. The steering wheel integrates haptic touch controls alongside the iconic physical Manettino dial, allowing seamless switching between eDrive, Hybrid, Performance, and Qualify modes without ever removing your hands from the leather-and-carbon rim.

## The High-End Hybrid Supercar Landscape

If you are shopping in the upper atmosphere of ultra-performance machinery in 2025, the hybrid landscape has never been more competitive. Here is how the reinvented SF90 compares to its closest rivals:

* **Ferrari SF90 XX Stradale (Approx. $890,000):** The limited-edition, track-focused apex of the SF90 family, boasting aggressive fixed aero wings and calibrated track-day performance.
* **Lamborghini Revuelto (Approx. $608,000):** Sant'Agata's naturally aspirated 6.5-liter V12 hybrid monster delivering 1,001 horsepower with an evocative soundtrack.
* **McLaren 750S (Approx. $345,000):** Pure combustion purity with a twin-turbo V8, offering hypercar-rivaling lightness and driver purity at a lower entry price.
* **Aston Martin Valhalla (Approx. $800,000):** A mid-engine PHEV developed with Formula 1 insights, packing 998 horsepower from an AMG-derived twin-turbo V8 and twin electric motors.

While rivals offer formidable performance, the SF90 remains the benchmark for everyday usability combined with brutal, four-digit horsepower figures.

## Our Verdict: The Bottom Line

The reinvented Ferrari SF90 is a triumph of modern engineering. Rather than signaling the demise of traditional supercar thrills, Maranello has demonstrated how electrification can amplify emotional engagement, speed, and precision. It combines the vicious, spine-tingling acoustic roar of an Italian V8 with the surgical precision of modern electric all-wheel drive.

For affluent collectors and serious performance drivers, the SF90 platform is not just a high-tech milestone for 2025—it is the definitive blueprint for the future of the supercar.

## Електрифікована силова установка V8: понад 1000 кінських сил

У механічному серці цього оновленого звіра б'ється вдосконалений 4,0-літровий двигун V8 із кутом розвалу 90 градусів і подвійним турбонаддувом, що працює в парі з трьома високопродуктивними електродвигунами з осьовим магнітним потоком. В останній конфігурації сумарна потужність системи перевищує вражаючі 1016 кінських сил, спрямовуючи чистий адреналін на всі чотири колеса через надшвидку восьмиступінчасту трансмісію з подвійним зчепленням.

Що робить цю силову установку по-справжньому особливою, так це миттєва віддача крутного моменту на низьких обертах. Два електродвигуни незалежно приводять у рух передню вісь, тоді як третій мотор, запозичений безпосередньо з технології RAC-e (Rotational Axis Control Electric) болідів Ferrari Formula 1, розташований між двигуном і коробкою передач. Результатом є бездоганна реакція на педаль газу, яка повністю нівелює турбояму, прискорюючи SF90 від 0 до 60 миль/год (0–97 км/год) усього за неймовірні 2,1 секунди.

Крім того, оновлена літій-іонна батарея ємністю 7,9 кВт·год забезпечує безшумний повністю електричний режим eDrive із запасом ходу приблизно 25 км (15 миль). Цього цілком достатньо, щоб непомітно виїхати зі спального району, перш ніж дати волю люті твін-турбо на відкритій трасі.
