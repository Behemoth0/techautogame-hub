## Introduction

Larian Studios redefined the computer role-playing game (CRPG) landscape with *Baldur's Gate 3*. With its branching narratives, memorable companion romances, cinematic presentation, and deep D&D 5th Edition turn-based combat, BG3 captured the hearts of millions. However, after hundreds of hours spent exploring Faerûn and curing mind-flayer parasites, every adventurer eventually reaches the end of the road.

If you are searching for that next great gaming high in 2025, you are in luck. The CRPG genre is currently enjoying a glorious golden age. Whether you crave complex dice rolls, deep tactical battles, or choice-driven storytelling, here are the 10 best games like *Baldur's Gate 3* you should add to your library today.

## 1. Divinity: Original Sin 2 ($44.99)

Before Larian Studios made BG3, they crafted *Divinity: Original Sin 2*, a absolute masterpiece that laid the mechanical foundation for Baldur's Gate. Set in the world of Rivellon, DOS2 offers unmatched environmental interactivity—you can combine fire with oil, electrify water surfaces, or turn blood into poison clouds.

While it uses Larian's proprietary system rather than D&D rules, the origin companion stories, deep character customization, and drop-in co-op multiplayer make it the closest spiritual sibling to BG3. It is mandatory playing for any Larian fan.

## 2. Solasta: Crown of the Magister ($29.99)

If what you loved most about BG3 was the strict adherence to Dungeons & Dragons 5e rules, *Solasta: Crown of the Magister* is your dream game. Built directly on the SRD 5.1 ruleset, Solasta faithfully translates 5e mechanics like vertical movement, light mechanics, cover, and tabletop dice rolls into digital form.

While its graphics and narrative presentation are more modest compared to BG3's blockbuster budget, its tactical combat is undeniably sharp and authentic to real tabletop sessions.

## 3. Pathfinder: Wrath of the Righteous ($39.99)

Developed by Owlcat Games, *Pathfinder: Wrath of the Righteous* is an epic fantasy CRPG based on the Pathfinder 1e system. You play as a hero leading a crusade against demonic forces, gaining powerful Mythic Paths that dramatically alter your character's power and the story's trajectory.

Pathfinder offers unprecedented build diversity with hundreds of classes, sub-classes, and feats. It features both real-time with pause and turn-based combat modes, making it ideal for players who love deep min-maxing and grand-scale fantasy wars.

## 4. Warhammer 40,000: Rogue Trader ($49.99)

Prefer grimdark science fiction over high fantasy? Owlcat Games applied their CRPG expertise to the Warhammer 40k universe in *Rogue Trader*. As a powerful Rogue Trader, you command a massive voidship, explore uncharted sectors of space, manage planetary colonies, and lead a squad of eccentric companions.

Featuring strategic turn-based cover shooter mechanics and moral choices that align you with Dogmatic, Iconoclast, or Heretical alignments, Rogue Trader is a deep narrative feast for sci-fi RPG lovers.

## 5. Dragon Age: Origins ($19.99)

BioWare's classic fantasy RPG served as a major spiritual inspiration for BG3's cinematic companion dialogues and dark fantasy tone. Set in the realm of Thedas, *Dragon Age: Origins* puts you in the boots of a Grey Warden tasked with stopping an apocalyptic invasion called the Blight.

Despite being released over a decade ago, its tactical pause-and-play combat, incredible companion chemistry, and weighty moral decisions hold up remarkably well in 2025.

## 6. Pillars of Eternity II: Deadfire ($39.99)

Obsidian Entertainment's *Pillars of Eternity II: Deadfire* takes the classic isometric RPG format and sets sail across a tropical archipelago. You play as the Watcher, chasing a rogue deity across the seas while managing your ship, crew, and faction allegiances.

Deadfire features exquisite hand-painted environments, rich lore, and the option to switch between real-time with pause and a dedicated turn-based tactical mode that feels very comfortable for BG3 veterans.

## 7. Wasteland 3 ($39.99)

For fans who enjoy BG3's tactical combat but want a post-apocalyptic spin, *Wasteland 3* is an exceptional choice. Set in a frozen, dystopian Colorado, you command a team of Desert Rangers trying to restore order in a chaotic land.

The game offers grid-based tactical combat similar to XCOM, dark humor, deep squad customization, and complex moral dilemmas where your decisions radically shape the fate of entire settlements.

## 8. Disco Elysium: The Final Cut ($39.99)

While *Disco Elysium* completely lacks traditional combat, it shares BG3's commitment to incredible choice-driven roleplaying and skill checks. You play as a disgraced detective suffering from amnesia trying to solve a murder case in a retro-futuristic city.

Your character's skills represent internal personality traits that talk directly to you. If your favorite part of BG3 was passing complex dialogue checks and discovering unique narrative paths, Disco Elysium is an absolute must-play masterpiece.

## 9. Marvel's Midnight Suns ($59.99)

Looking for great tactical turn-based party combat paired with rich downtime companion bonding? *Marvel's Midnight Suns* blends deckbuilding tactics with RPG social simulation. Between missions, you hang out at the Abbey with iconic Marvel heroes like Blade, Iron Man, and Ghost Rider, building friendships that unlock tactical bonuses in combat.

## 10. Dragon Age: The Veilguard ($69.99)

The newest entry in the iconic franchise, *Dragon Age: The Veilguard* delivers a flashy, action-oriented party RPG experience with Hollywood-level cinematic production. While combat shifts toward real-time action, its emphasis on party banter, vibrant worlds, and saving the realm from ancient threats will feel comfortably familiar to BG3 fans.

## Bottom Line / Our Verdict

If you want the closest possible match to *Baldur's Gate 3* in terms of gameplay mechanics and developer philosophy, **Divinity: Original Sin 2 ($44.99)** remains the undisputed top recommendation. For players who specifically crave strict D&D rule accuracy, **Solasta: Crown of the Magister ($29.99)** is your best bet. Finally, if you want unmatched depth and complex character building, jump straight into **Pathfinder: Wrath of the Righteous ($39.99)**.

Як можутьній Вольний Торговець (Rogue Trader), ви командуєте величезним космічним кораблем, досліджуєте незвідані сектори космосу, керуєте планетарними колоніями та очолюєте загін ексцентричних супутників.
