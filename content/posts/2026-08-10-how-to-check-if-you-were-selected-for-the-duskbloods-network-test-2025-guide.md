## Step-by-Step: How to Check Your Selection Status

Unlike traditional open beta distributions, selection codes for The Duskbloods are tied directly to your registered publisher account and platform ID. Here is how you can verify your entry status right now:

### 1. Check Your Email Inbox (And Spam Folder)
Official invitations are being dispatched from the official publisher domain. Search your inbox for emails titled **'The Duskbloods Network Test Selection Notice'** or **'Your Invitation to the Nightfall Realm'**.
* **Pro Tip:** Make sure to inspect your 'Promotions', 'Spam', and 'Junk' folders. Automated invite keys are frequently flagged by aggressive email filters.

### 2. Check the Official Account Portal
If you cannot find an email, do not panic! You can check your account status directly via the developer portal:
1. Navigate to the official publisher website and log in using the credentials you used during registration.
2. Go to your **Account Settings** or **Beta Participation** tab.
3. Look under **Active Playtests**. If selected, a 12-digit platform code or direct platform redeem button will be visible next to *The Duskbloods Network Test*.

### 3. Check Console Message Notifications
For PS5 and Xbox Series X users, keep an eye on your console notifications. In certain regions, platform accounts receive direct system messages containing pre-load links once access is granted.

## What to Do If You Received an Invite Code

If you were selected, congratulations! The pre-load period begins 24 hours prior to the first test session. Here is how to redeem your code on your respective platform:

* **PlayStation 5 / PS5 Pro:** Open the PlayStation Store > Select the '...' menu at the top right > Click **Redeem Code** > Enter your 12-digit code.
* **Xbox Series X|S:** Press the Xbox button > Select Store > Scroll down the left sidebar to **Redeem** > Enter your 25-character code.
* **Steam (PC):** Open Steam > Click **Games** on the top menu > Select **Activate a Product on Steam...** > Follow the on-screen prompts.

### 2. Перевірте офіційний портал облікового запису

Користувачам PS5 та Xbox Series X варто стежити за системними сповіщеннями на консолі. У деяких регіонах після надання доступу надходять системні повідомлення із посиланням на попереднє завантаження.
