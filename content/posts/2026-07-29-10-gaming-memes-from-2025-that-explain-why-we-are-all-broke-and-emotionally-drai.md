## Welcome to 2025: Where Everything Has AI and Nothing Works

Congratulations, everyone! We made it to 2025. We were promised flying cars, cyberpunk utopias, and full-immersion VR. Instead, we got smart fridges that require subscription payments to unlock the ice dispenser and graphics cards that cost more than a 2012 Honda Civic with 180,000 miles on it.

If you have spent any time on PCMasterRace, Twitter, or Discord this year, you know that 2025 has been an absolute goldmine for top-tier coping mechanisms—otherwise known as memes. Here is a breakdown of the funniest tech and gaming memes that hit way too close to home this year.

## The Nvidia RTX 5090: Comes with a Free Mortgage Application

Nvidia finally dropped the RTX 5090 this year, and the memes instantly wrote themselves. The card is so large it requires its own support beam, an updated electrical panel for your house, and a signed letter of consent from your bank manager. Jensen Huang popped on stage in a new leather jacket and basically told us that selling a kidney is now an official payment option at checkout.

Here are 3 observations on buying a GPU in 2025:

1. The power cable doesn't just plug into your power supply anymore; it wire-taps directly into your neighborhood's main power grid.
2. Gamers are buying $2,000 graphics cards just to play pixel-art indie games about farming radishes at 1,400 FPS.
3. Scalpers are no longer accepting cash; they are asking for physical gold bullion and blood sacrifices.

## Windows 12: Microsoft Copilot Is Now Watching You Sleep

Microsoft decided that Windows 11 wasn't intrusive enough, so they rolled out Windows 12 with supercharged 'Recall AI' integrated into every single millisecond of your existence. The memes about Windows 12 literally feeling like an overattached ex have dominated tech forums all year.

You open Chrome? Copilot asks if you want an AI-generated poem about browsing habits. You miss a headshot in Valorant? Copilot pops up in the corner saying, 'It looks like you suck at aiming. Would you like me to write an apology letter to your teammates?'

3 things Microsoft thinks gamers want vs. what we actually want:

1. Microsoft thinks: An AI assistant that predicts what game you want to play based on your heart rate.
2. Gamers actually want: A control panel that doesn't look like it was designed by three different committees in 1998, 2012, and 2025.
3. Gamers actually want: The ability to delete Edge without Windows acting like you just kicked its puppy.

## The Steam Pile of Shame Has Reached Sentience

We all thought we were going to conquer our backlogs in 2025. We were wrong. The Steam Summer Sale hit, Gaben took our wallets, and now our digital libraries look like an unread email inbox from a CEO who retired five years ago. The biggest meme of 2025 is the realization that 'buying games' and 'playing games' are two completely separate, unrelated hobbies.

Here are 4 stages of buying a game on Steam in 2025:

1. Wishlist a game because the trailer looks neat (2022).
2. Buy the game on a 85% discount for $4.99 (2025).
3. Install the game, stare at the desktop icon for four seconds, and feel a deep sense of accomplishment.
4. Close Steam and play Old School RuneScape or Skyrim for the 4,000th hour.

## Discord's UI Designers Need to Be Stopped

Why does Discord feel the need to redesign its interface every three business days? Every time you log in, the mute button has migrated to a different quadrant of the screen. The memes comparing Discord's UI team to bored cats knocking glasses off tables have been running rampant.

3 things Discord updates before fixing basic screen-share audio:

1. Adding a $10 custom profile banner that sparkles when you ignore your friends.
2. Moving the settings gear icon to inside the text chat box just to confuse your muscle memory.
3. Adding an AI bot that tells you your microphone sounds like a helicopter landing on a bag of potato chips.

## Bottom Line

2025 has proven one thing: tech keeps getting faster, pricier, and weirder, but gamer humor remains unchanged. We laugh at these memes to keep from crying over our bank accounts and RAM usage. 

Real talk: don't take out a loan for a 5090, don't let Copilot ruin your life, and for the love of Gaben, play at least ONE game in your Steam backlog before buying three more.
