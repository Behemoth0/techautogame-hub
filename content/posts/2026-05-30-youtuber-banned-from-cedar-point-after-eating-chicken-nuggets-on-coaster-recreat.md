## The Stunt Heard 'Round the Internet

Rollercoasters and loose objects do not mix. Cedar Point, located in Sandusky, Ohio, is known as the "Roller Coaster Capital of the World" and enforces some of the strictest safety policies in the amusement industry. For good reason: at speeds exceeding 90 mph, a flying chicken nugget—or worse, the heavy action camera used to film the stunt—can become a lethal projectile for riders sitting behind you.

According to reports, the YouTuber used a concealed body-mount rig to sneak his camera past ride operators, along with a pocket full of nuggets. Mid-drop, he pulled out the food, attempting to swallow a nugget while experiencing sustained G-forces. Park security was waiting at the exit gates. 

This incident highlights a growing tension in 2025 between theme parks and creators pushing the envelope for viral engagement. Fortunately, with modern gaming technology, you don't need to risk a lifetime ban (or your life) to build, ride, and film extreme coasters.

2. **Approved Mounts:** Never hand-hold a camera or phone on a ride. Use secure, park-approved wrist or chest mounts if permitted.
