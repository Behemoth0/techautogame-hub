## Introduction

If you hear a creepy, mechanical voice asking, "Do you want to play a game?" later this month, it won't be coming from your Netflix queue. In a massive blow to horror fans, Netflix is officially losing the licensing rights to the entire *Saw* franchise. From the low-budget, mind-bending original masterpiece to the elaborate, blood-soaked sequels, John Kramer and his apprentices are packing up their reverse bear traps and leaving the streaming giant.

For film buffs, this means a mad scramble to binge-watch all ten movies before the countdown clock hits zero. But for gamers, this departure is a reminder of how deeply the *Saw* franchise has influenced the survival horror genre. If you want to keep the adrenaline pumping long after the credits roll on Netflix, there are plenty of ways to transition from passive viewer to active participant. Here is why you need to start bingeing now, and how you can keep the spirit of Jigsaw alive on your gaming rig in 2025.

## Why the Loss of Saw on Netflix Hurts (and Why You Need to Binge Now)

The *Saw* franchise is more than just "torture porn"—it is a masterclass in serialized, soap-opera-style horror storytelling. Watching the narrative threads weave together across multiple films is incredibly satisfying, making it the perfect candidate for a weekend marathon. 

With the movies leaving Netflix, horror fans are losing one of the most accessible ways to experience this legendary run. Licensing agreements in 2025 are becoming increasingly fractured, meaning the franchise will likely be scattered across various premium rental platforms. If you want to experience the complete timeline without paying per-movie rental fees, your window of opportunity is closing fast.

But once you finish your marathon, what comes next? Fortunately, the gaming world has embraced the industrial, anxiety-inducing vibes of *Saw* with open arms. 

## Bringing Jigsaw to Your Console: The Ultimate Gaming Alternatives

If watching Jigsaw's victims struggle to escape their traps makes your palms sweat, playing through similar scenarios will push your heart rate into the red zone. The gaming industry has spent years perfecting the art of the "escape room with consequences," and several titles capture the exact essence of the *Saw* films.

To help you transition from the silver screen to the gaming screen, we have rounded up the best games, DLCs, and gear to keep the horror alive.

### 1. Dead by Daylight: SAW Chapter DLC
* **Approximate Price:** $6.99

If you want the most direct crossover possible, Behaviour Interactive's asymmetrical multiplayer horror game *Dead by Daylight* has you covered. The *SAW Chapter* DLC introduces Amanda Young (known in-game as "The Pig") as a playable Killer, alongside Detective David Tapp as a Survivor. The DLC also features the iconic Gideon Meat Plant map. Playing as The Pig allows you to place Reverse Bear Traps on Survivors' heads, forcing them to search jigsaw-box stations around the map before their timers expire and their jaws are ripped open. It is a brilliant, terrifying translation of the film's core mechanic into competitive gameplay.

### 2. The Outlast Trials
* **Approximate Price:** $39.99

If you want a game that captures the grime, the industrial aesthetic, and the sheer, hopeless panic of being trapped in a sadistic experiment, Red Barrels' *The Outlast Trials* is the ultimate 2025 pick. Set during the Cold War, players are forced to navigate terrifying, trap-filled environments designed by the Murkoff Corporation. Whether you play solo or in co-op, you must solve puzzles, avoid horrific enemies, and survive trials that feel like they were designed by Jigsaw's most twisted disciples. The sound design alone is enough to induce a panic attack.

### 3. Saw: 10-Film Collection (Blu-ray)
* **Approximate Price:** $24.99

Let's face it: streaming platforms cannot be trusted to keep our favorite films forever. The sudden departure of the franchise from Netflix is the perfect excuse to invest in physical media. The *Saw: 10-Film Collection* on Blu-ray ensures you always have access to John Kramer's moral crusades, uncompressed audio, and plenty of behind-the-scenes featurettes. In an era of digital impermanence, owning the physical discs is the only way to guarantee your collection remains intact.

### 4. SteelSeries Arctis Nova 7 Wireless Headset
* **Approximate Price:** $179.99

To truly immerse yourself in survival horror games, you need a gaming headset that delivers pin-point spatial audio. The *SteelSeries Arctis Nova 7* is one of the best mid-range wireless headsets on the market in 2025. When playing games like *The Outlast Trials* or *Dead by Daylight*, hearing the distant click of a trap setting, the heavy breathing of a killer around the corner, or the mechanical whir of a Jigsaw box is crucial to your survival. The dual-wireless connectivity also means you can connect to your console and your phone simultaneously, just in case you need to look up a puzzle guide on YouTube without pausing the game.

## The Evolution of Escape-Room Horror in 2025

The departure of *Saw* from Netflix marks the end of an era for casual streaming, but it also highlights how much the horror landscape has evolved. In 2025, horror is no longer a passive experience. Gamers are looking for interactive tension, high stakes, and complex puzzle-solving that mirrors the psychological weight of the films.

As you prepare to say goodbye to the films on your streaming dashboard, remember that the spirit of survival horror is stronger than ever. Whether you are looping killers as Detective Tapp in *Dead by Daylight* or screaming with your friends in *The Outlast Trials*, the legacy of Jigsaw's twisted games is safe in the hands of the gaming community.

## Our Verdict: The Bottom Line

Don't wait until the end of the month to regret missing out. **Our recommendation is simple: start your Netflix binge-watch immediately.** Watch the original trilogy to appreciate the tight, low-budget filmmaking, and then dive into the later entries for the absurdly creative traps.

Once the credits roll on *Saw X*, make the jump to the virtual world. Grab the **Dead by Daylight: SAW Chapter DLC** for a quick, affordable hit of nostalgia, or invest in **The Outlast Trials** if you want a modern, heart-pounding multiplayer experience that captures the true essence of Jigsaw’s industrial nightmare. Game over? Not even close.
