## The Ultimate Southern Road Trip Upgrade

Picture this: It’s a crisp morning in 2025, and you’re cruising down Interstate 85 in your electric vehicle. Your battery indicator dips below 20%, and your stomach is rumbling. In the past, this meant two separate stops—one at a bleak, concrete charging station behind a shopping mall, and another at a fast-food drive-thru. But today, you pull into a brightly lit Bojangles parking lot. You plug into a high-powered DC fast charger, walk inside for a warm Cajun Filet Biscuit and a cup of Legendary Iced Tea, and by the time you've finished your meal, your EV is topped up to 80% and ready to hit the road.

This isn't a futuristic fantasy; it’s the new reality of EV road-tripping. Bojangles, the beloved Southeastern fast-food chain famous for its chicken and biscuits, is officially entering the EV charging space. By partnering with leading charging infrastructure providers, Bojangles is turning its restaurant parking lots into high-speed charging hubs. It’s a brilliant move that highlights a massive shift in how we think about refueling our vehicles in 2025.

## Why Fast Food and Fast Charging are a Perfect Match

For years, the biggest complaint about electric vehicles has been the "waiting game." While pumping gas takes five minutes, charging an EV at a public Level 2 station can take hours. Even with modern DC fast chargers, you're looking at a 20-to-40-minute wait to get a substantial charge.

This is where the "dwell time" concept comes in. What do you do for 30 minutes while your car charges? You eat.

Bojangles’ strategy exploits this perfect synergy. By installing DC fast chargers (typically ranging from 150 kW to 350 kW), they can add 100+ miles of range to most modern EVs in the exact amount of time it takes to order, sit down, eat a hot meal, and use the restroom. It transforms a logistical chore into a convenient lunch break. For Bojangles, it's a guaranteed way to drive foot traffic and increase average ticket sizes. For EV drivers, it's a welcome oasis of comfort and Southern hospitality.

## The Infrastructure Shift of 2025

We are witnessing a quiet revolution in the charging landscape. The era of the isolated charging station is ending. Drivers no longer want to sit in dark, empty parking lots at midnight waiting for their batteries to charge. They want amenities, safety, and hot food.

By integrating fast chargers into established retail and dining locations, brands like Bojangles are helping democratize EV ownership in the Southeast—a region that has historically lagged behind the West Coast in EV adoption. This expansion is supported by both private capital and federal funding from the National Electric Vehicle Infrastructure (NEVI) Formula Program, which aims to build a coast-to-coast network of fast chargers.

## Overcoming the "Charger Desert" in the South

The Southeastern United States has long been considered a challenging region for EV road trips due to sparser charging infrastructure compared to the Northeast or California. Bojangles' footprint—spanning across states like North Carolina, South Carolina, Georgia, and Tennessee—positions them perfectly to fill these critical gaps.

By placing chargers along major highway corridors and in suburban hubs, Bojangles is helping build the "range confidence" that local drivers need to make the switch to electric. It’s a win-win for regional tourism, local economies, and the environment.

The partnership between fast-food giants like Bojangles and EV charging networks is a glimpse into the future of travel. It proves that the transition to electric vehicles isn't just about changing what's under the hood—it's about reimagining the entire travel experience.

Bojangles is smart to realize that hungry EV drivers are a captive audience. By offering fast, reliable charging alongside their iconic biscuits, they are setting a standard that we hope other regional fast-food chains copy immediately. Our verdict? Next time you plan a Southern road trip, map your route around a Bojangles. Your stomach—and your EV's battery—will thank you.

## Чому швидке харчування та швидка зарядка — ідеальна пара

Протягом багатьох років найбільшою скаргою на електромобілі було «очікування». Тоді як заправка бензином займає п'ять хвилин, зарядка електромобіля на публічній станції Level 2 може тривати годинами. Навіть із сучасними швидкими зарядками постійного струму (DC) вам доведеться чекати від 20 до 40 хвилин, щоб отримати суттєвий рівень заряду.

Саме тут у гр�� вступає концепція «часу перебування» (dwell time). Що ви робите протягом 30 хвилин, поки ваш автомобіль заряджається? Ви їсте.

Стратегія Bojangles використовує цю ідеальну синергію. Це перетворює логістичну рутину на зручну обідню перерву. Для Bojangles це гарантований спосіб залучити клієнтів та збільшити середній чек.
