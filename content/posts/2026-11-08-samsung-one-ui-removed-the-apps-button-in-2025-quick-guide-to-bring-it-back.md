## The Disappearing Apps Button: What Happened in One UI?

If you have recently unboxed a new Samsung phone or updated your current device to the latest One UI build in 2025, you might have felt an immediate twinge of muscle memory betrayal. You glance at your home screen dock expecting to tap that trusty grid icon to open your full catalog of applications, only to find an empty slot—or worse, a forced app shortcut you didn't ask for.

Like many modern Android skins, Samsung has spent the last few iterations nudging users toward gesture navigation. By default, One UI now expects you to swipe vertically anywhere on the home screen to access your App Drawer. While fluid swipes make sense on paper, millions of long-time Galaxy loyalists still prefer a dedicated, physical-feeling target to tap.

The good news? Samsung didn't permanently kill the feature; they just buried the toggle in an unassuming settings menu. Whether you are rocking an ultra-premium flagship or a budget daily driver, you can bring that beloved button back in less than thirty seconds.

## Step-by-Step: How to Restore the Apps Button on Your Galaxy Phone

You do not need root access, third-party launchers, or custom software to fix this. The toggle lives right inside native One UI settings.

1. **Long-Press the Home Screen:** Tap and hold any blank area on your home screen until the layout zoom-out animation triggers.
2. **Open Settings:** Tap the **Settings** gear icon in the bottom right corner of the screen.
3. **Locate the Toggle:** Scroll through the Home Screen settings menu until you find **Show Apps screen button on Home screen**.
4. **Toggle It On:** Switch the toggle from gray to blue.
5. **Return Home:** Tap your back button or swipe to return to your main interface.

Immediately upon returning, you will notice a 3x3 dot grid icon anchored at the far right of your bottom dock. Tapping it will reliably jump straight into your app drawer, exactly the way Samsung phones functioned for years.

## Why Some Users Still Prefer the Dedicated Button Over Swiping

Gesture navigation is great, but touch targets still have functional advantages. For one, users with accessibility needs or motor control difficulties often find swiping unreliable compared to a clear, stationary touchpoint.

There is also the friction of screen clutter. When you pack your home screen with interactive widgets—like real-time stock tickers, Spotify controllers, or calendar views—attempting to swipe up often accidentally activates a widget instead of launching your drawer. Having a dedicated Apps button eliminates accidental inputs entirely.

Furthermore, if you use a rugged vehicle mount while driving, swiping up while your phone is docked on your dash can be clumsy and distracting. A single tap to bring up navigation or audio apps is significantly safer and more tactile.

## Advanced One UI Customization: Good Lock & Home Up

Once you restore the Apps button, you might want to take your home screen organization even further. Samsung’s official suite of power-user tools, available through the Galaxy Store under the app name **Good Lock**, lets you overhaul nearly every interface element.

Specifically, look for the **Home Up** module inside Good Lock. This module allows you to:

* **Change App Drawer Scrolling:** Switch your app drawer from horizontal paging to an endless vertical scroll (similar to stock Google Pixel).
* **Adjust Dock Density:** Expand your dock from 5 apps up to 7, letting you keep your Apps button alongside all your essential daily shortcuts.
* **Hide App Labels:** Create a clean, minimalist home screen by stripping away the text under icons while keeping the Apps button intact.

## Our Verdict

Tech companies often rush to simplify interfaces by eliminating buttons in favor of gestures, but real-world ergonomics don't always align with design minimalism. Re-enabling the dedicated Apps button restores immediate visual certainty and tactile speed to your workflow. If you have been struggling with accidental widget taps or miss the classic Android feel, taking thirty seconds to toggle this setting back on will make your phone feel like home again.

## Покрокова інструкція: як відновити кнопку програм на смартфоні Galaxy

Вам не потрібні root-права, сторонні лаунчери чи додаткове програмне забезпечення. Потрібний перемикач знаходиться безпосередньо у стандартних налаштуваннях One UI.

1. **Затисніть головний екран:** Торкніться будь-якого вільного місця на головному екрані й утримуйте палець, доки не з'явиться меню редагування сітки.
2. **Відкрийте налаштування:** Натисніть на значок шестірні **«Налаштування»** в нижньому правому кутку екрана.
3. **Знайдіть потрібний пункт:** Прокрутіть меню налаштувань головного екрана, доки не побачите параметр **«Кнопка екрана програм на головному екрані»**.
4. **Увімкніть його:** Переведіть перемикач у положення «увімкнено» (з сірого в синій колір).
5. **Поверніться назад:** Натисніть кнопку «Назад» або зробіть жест повернення на головний екран.

Одразу після повернення ви помітите іконку у вигляді сітки з крапок 3x3 у крайньому правому кутку нижньої панелі. Натискання на неї одразу відкриватиме екран усіх програм — точнісінько так, як це працювало на смартфонах Samsung роками.
