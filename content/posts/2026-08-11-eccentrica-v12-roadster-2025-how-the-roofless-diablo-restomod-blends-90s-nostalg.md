## Introduction: The Revival of a Legend—Now Open-Air

Few supercars define the wild, unbridled energy of the 1990s quite like the Lamborghini Diablo. With its wedge shape, roaring naturally aspirated V12, and notoriously challenging driver feedback, the original Diablo was as intimidating as it was mesmerizing. Fast forward to 2025, and San Marino-based startup Eccentrica has taken this legendary supercar to an entirely new dimension with the unveil of the Eccentrica V12 Roadster.

Following the success of their hardtop restomod, the V12 Roadster strips away the roof to deliver an visceral open-air driving experience. However, cutting the roof off a 500-plus horsepower mid-engine classic is no small feat. In the era of modern supercar performance, structural integrity and driver safety take center stage. Here is a deep dive into how Eccentrica modernizes a 90s icon with structural safety engineering, upgraded dynamics, and essential safety accessories every high-performance restomod enthusiast should consider.

## Engineering Safety into a Roofless 90s Icon

Converting a classic coupe into a roadster presents massive engineering hurdles, particularly regarding vehicle safety and chassis flex. Original 1990s supercars lacked the rigorous crash structures and torsional rigidity standards we demand today. When you remove the roof of a monocoque or tubular spaceframe chassis, structural rigidity drops dramatically, which negatively impacts handling, rollover safety, and occupant protection.

To overcome this, Eccentrica engineered a extensive structural reinforcement package. The V12 Roadster utilizes composite materials, strategically integrating high-modulus carbon fiber along the sills, floor pan, and rear bulkhead. This targeted stiffening restores structural rigidity without adding unnecessary weight.

Furthermore, open-top supercars require specialized rollover protection. Eccentrica incorporated reinforced windshield pillars (A-pillars) and hidden rollover protection structures behind the headrests. These safety enhancements ensure that despite its retro roots, the V12 Roadster offers structural resilience far exceeding its 1990s donor car.

## Modern Stopping Power, Suspension, and Visibility

Safety in a 550+ horsepower manual V12 supercar is not just about surviving a crash—it is about avoiding one entirely. The original Diablo was infamous for stiff pedal feel, lack of ABS in early models, and compromised rearward visibility. Eccentrica addresses these active safety hazards with comprehensive hardware upgrades:

1. Enhanced Braking Systems: The V12 Roadster replaces dated factory calipers with motorsport-grade Brembo braking systems featuring lightweight monobloc calipers and oversized slotted discs. This drastically reduces stopping distances and eliminates brake fade during aggressive driving.
2. Modernized Lighting Technology: The iconic pop-up headlights have been redesigned with custom pop-down LED headlights. Modern LED projectors provide dramatically superior illumination range and beam precision compared to original halogen bulbs, significantly improving night-driving visibility and safety.
3. Re-engineered Suspension Dynamics: Active TracTive semi-active dampers adjust in real-time, maintaining optimal tire contact patches over uneven surfaces. This modern suspension setup reduces unpredictable mid-corner snap oversteer—a historic hazard of early mid-engine supercars.

## Essential Aftermarket Safety Tech for Open-Top Restomods

While the Eccentrica V12 Roadster comes built to bespoke perfection, owners of classic supercars and high-end restomods often need supplemental aftermarket technology to ensure maximum situational awareness and vehicle security. If you are piloting an open-top high-performance machine in 2025, here are four top-tier safety devices worth adding to your setup:

### 1. Thinkware U3000 Dual Dash Cam (~$549)
Open-top supercars attract significant attention, both good and bad. The Thinkware U3000 offers 4K UHD front recording and 2K QHD rear recording, powered by Sony STARVIS 2 image sensors. Crucially, its built-in Radar Parking Mode detects motion and impact while parked, consuming minimal power. For a rare restomod like the Eccentrica, having clear video evidence in case of vandalism or parking lot mishaps is invaluable.

### 2. Escort MAX 360c MKII Radar Detector (~$699)
Situational awareness is critical when operating a raw V12 vehicle. The Escort MAX 360c MKII features dual-antenna directional arrows, long-range detection, and false-alert filtering powered by auto-learning AI. By keeping drivers aware of surrounding traffic patterns and speed enforcement zones, it encourages safer, more focused driving habits.

### 3. FOBO Tire 2 Smart TPMS (~$149)
Classic supercars rarely featured tire pressure monitoring systems (TPMS), yet high-speed tire blowouts are one of the most severe hazards on open roads. The FOBO Tire 2 system uses Bluetooth 5.0 sensors attached directly to your tire valves, transmitting real-time pressure and temperature readings to your smartphone or smartwatch before you even roll out of the garage.

### 4. Brembo GT-R Big Brake Kit (~$8,500 - $11,000)
For restomod builders upgrading their own classic supercars, replacing factory brakes with a Brembo GT-R system is the ultimate safety upgrade. Featuring nickel-plated billet aluminum calipers and two-piece floating discs, this setup provides incredible thermal resistance and linear pedal response under extreme conditions.

## Balancing Raw Mechanical Thrills with Modern Safety Standards

The ultimate appeal of the Eccentrica V12 Roadster lies in its balance. Modern supercars are heavily insulated, brimming with invasive electronic driver aids like electronic stability control, lane-keep assist, and automatic emergency braking. While these systems save lives in daily commuters, they can dilute the pure, unadulterated sensation of driving a manual V12.

Eccentrica preserves the tactile heart of the original vehicle while applying structural, mechanical, and safety updates where they matter most. By reinforcing the chassis frame, refining the suspension, fitting modern tires, and upgrading the brakes, the vehicle becomes vastly safer to drive hard without losing its soul. It proves that safety in the restomod realm does not have to mean sacrificing visceral engagement; rather, it creates a stable platform that allows drivers to explore high performance with confidence.

## Bottom Line / Our Verdict

The Eccentrica V12 Roadster is a masterclass in how to modernize an analog supercar for 2025. By pairing aggressive 90s aesthetic styling with high-rigidity carbon reinforcements, state-of-the-art Brembo braking, and advanced LED optics, Eccentrica has successfully mitigated the inherent safety drawbacks of the original Lamborghini Diablo Roadster.

For enthusiasts looking to preserve classic driving sensations while prioritizing safety, restomods like the Eccentrica set the gold standard. When supplemented with modern aftermarket safety gadgets like smart TPMS and 4K dash cams, driving a high-powered open-top restomod can be as secure as it is exhilarating.

Перетворення класичного купе на родстер супроводжується величезними інженерними викликами, особливо щодо безпеки автомобіля та жорсткості шасі. ## Сучасне гальмування, підвіска та оглядовість

Безпека у суперкарі з V12 та механічною коробкою передач потужністю 550+ к. с. полягає не лише у виживанні під час аварії, а й у її запобіганні. Оригінальний Diablo славився тугою педаллю гальм��, відсутністю ABS у ранніх версіях та поганим оглядом назад. Eccentrica вирішує ці ризики за допомогою комплексного оновлення обладнання:

1. Покращені гальмівні системи: V12 Roadster замінює застарілі заводські супорти на спортивну гальмівну систему Brembo з легкими моноблочними супортами та збільшеними перфорованими дисками. Це кардинально скорочує гальмівний шлях та усуває перегрів гальм під час агресивного водіння.
2. Модернізована світлотехніка: Легендарні висувні фари замінили на кастомні світлодіодні LED-фари із механізмом опускання. Сучасні світлодіодні проектори забезпечують значно більшу дальність та точність світлового променя порівняно з оригінальними галогенними лампами, істотно підвищуючи оглядовість і безпеку під час нічних поїздок.
