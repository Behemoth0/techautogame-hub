## Introduction

The tabletop roleplaying world has just received its biggest jolt of adrenaline for 2025. Critical Role, the global TTRPG phenomenon, has officially announced its next flagship campaign: *Age of Umbra: Sallowlands*. In a move that has sent shockwaves of excitement through the Critter community, the legendary Matthew Mercer is officially returning to the Dungeon Master's seat to guide this dark, mysterious new chapter.

After a period of experimentation with guest DMs, spin-off series, and anthology shows, Mercer’s return to the head of the table represents a homecoming for fans who fell in love with his intricate world-building, unmatched voice acting, and deep emotional storytelling. Here is everything you need to know about *Age of Umbra: Sallowlands*, what to expect from the new setting, and the essential gear you need to upgrade your own gaming table as we head into this exciting new era of tabletop gaming.

## Descending into the Sallowlands: What We Know So Far

While Campaign 3 (Bells Hells) pushed the boundaries of Exandria's lore, *Age of Umbra: Sallowlands* promises a major shift in tone and atmosphere. Scheduled to premiere in early 2025, the campaign is set in a previously unexplored, decaying continent known as the Sallowlands. Described by the production team as a "gothic, shadow-drenched fantasy noir," the setting departs from traditional high-fantasy heroism in favor of survival, moral ambiguity, and cosmic dread.

Rumors suggest that this campaign will heavily utilize Darrington Press’s proprietary *Daggerheart* system, alongside classic D&D elements, to create a more narrative-focused, high-stakes mechanical environment. The "Umbra" of the title refers to a creeping, magical eclipse that has permanently blanketed the Sallowlands in twilight, mutating the local wildlife and giving rise to terrifying new factions.

## The Mercer Magic: Why His Return Matters

Over the last decade, Matthew Mercer has become the gold standard for Dungeon Masters worldwide. His ability to juggle dozens of distinct NPCs, weave complex political intrigues, and foster a safe, collaborative space for his players has redefined what cooperative storytelling can be.

Having Mercer back at the helm for a long-form campaign ensures that *Age of Umbra* will feature the same depth of lore that made the continents of Tal'Dorei and Wildemount feel like living, breathing places. The Sallowlands setting gives Mercer a playground to flex his horror muscles—reminiscent of his work on the Briarwood arc, but expanded to an entire continent. For fans of deep lore, tragic character arcs, and nail-biting combat encounters, his return is the ultimate assurance of quality.

## Broadcasting Details and What to Expect

*Age of Umbra: Sallowlands* is expected to stream on Critical Role’s official Twitch and YouTube channels. While the exact cast lineup has not been fully disclosed, core cast members like Laura Bailey, Travis Willingham, and Ashley Johnson are expected to return, alongside some exciting guest players from the broader TTRPG community.

Production values are also getting a massive upgrade. Insider reports suggest a newly designed set featuring dynamic projection mapping and integrated smart-lighting to reflect the shifting, twilight atmosphere of the Sallowlands in real-time as the players explore.

## Bottom Line: Our Verdict

**Our Verdict:** *Age of Umbra: Sallowlands* is shaping up to be a monumental milestone for tabletop gaming in 2025. By returning Matthew Mercer to the DM screen and plunging the cast into a dark, gothic-noir setting, Critical Role is poised to capture the raw, emotional magic of their early campaigns while pushing the boundaries of modern production. Whether you are a veteran Critter or a newcomer looking for a fresh starting point, this is an absolute must-watch event that will undoubtedly influence home games for years to come.
