## From Pixels to Pages: The Grimdark Renaissance of 2025

For gamers, the wait for the next massive dark fantasy RPG is a familiar torment. With CD Projekt Red still deep in development on the next *Witcher* saga (Project Polaris) and Bethesda taking its sweet time with *The Elder Scrolls VI*, we find ourselves constantly chasing that high of political intrigue, morally grey protagonists, and terrifying monsters. 

Fortunately, the literary world is stepping up in 2025. Grimdark and dark fantasy are experiencing a massive renaissance, delivering the same blood-soaked, politically charged narratives that made *The Witcher* and *Game of Thrones* global phenomena. If you want to trade your controller for a paperback this year, here are the 10 best new dark fantasy books you need to add to your reading list.

---

## The 10 Best New Dark Fantasy Books in 2025

### 1. *The Devils* by Joe Abercrombie
* **Release Date:** May 2025
* **Approximate Price:** $29.99 (Hardcover Pre-order)

The undisputed king of modern grimdark, Joe Abercrombie, returns in 2025 with *The Devils*. Set in a dark, magic-ravaged alternative Europe, the story follows a band of self-serving, monstrous outcasts forced to work for the church to stop an even greater threat. If you loved the cynical humor of Geralt of Rivia and the dysfunctional squad dynamics of *The Witcher 3*'s DLCs, this is your most anticipated book of the year.

### 2. *Empire of the Damned* by Jay Kristoff
* **Approximate Price:** $20.99

While technically released in late 2024, Kristoff's epic vampire-hunting sequel is dominating 2025 reading lists. Following Gabriel de León—the last of a holy order of silversmiths—this book is essentially *The Witcher* meets *Castlevania*. It features incredible worldbuilding, brutal combat, and a post-apocalyptic fantasy world where the sun has failed and monsters rule the night.

### 3. *The Tainted Cup* by Robert Jackson Bennett
* **Approximate Price:** $18.00

If your favorite part of *The Witcher* is Geralt investigating monster attacks using his Witcher senses, *The Tainted Cup* is a must-read. It’s a dark fantasy detective novel set in an empire defended by massive, bio-engineered walls. When an imperial officer is found dead with a magical tree bursting from his chest, an eccentric investigator and his assistant must solve the conspiracy before the kingdom crumbles.

### 4. *The Strength of the Few* by James Islington
* **Approximate Price:** $19.99

Following up on his massive hit *The Will of the Many*, Islington delivers another masterclass in political intrigue and dark magic. Think *Game of Thrones* style backstabbing set in a brutal, Roman-inspired magical academy where the ruling class literally siphons the life force and willpower of the lower classes. It’s tense, cerebral, and deeply satisfying.

### 5. *The Silverblood Promise* by Gary Johnston
* **Approximate Price:** $18.99

This debut fantasy novel took the community by storm. It follows Lukan Shard, a disgraced nobleman turned scoundrel, who must delve into the dark underbelly of a decadent city to solve his father's murder. It features a fast-paced, mystery-driven plot that feels like a high-stakes questline straight out of *Novigrad*.

### 6. *A Fate Inked in Blood* by Danielle L. Jensen
* **Approximate Price:** $17.99

For fans who love the Norse mythology of *God of War* and the gritty shield-wall combat of *Vikings*, Jensen’s dark fantasy romance delivers in spades. It features magic bound by blood oaths, scheming warlords, and a brutal winter setting that will make you want to wrap yourself in a wolf pelt.

### 7. *The Trials of Empire* by Richard Swan
* **Approximate Price:** $18.99

This epic conclusion to the *Empire of the Wolf* trilogy is perfect for fans of *Game of Thrones*' political maneuvering and legal drama. Sir Konrad Vonvalt is an Emperor’s Justice—part detective, part judge, part executioner. As the empire descends into a horrific civil war fueled by demonic magic, Vonvalt must decide how much of his humanity he is willing to sacrifice to save the realm.

### 8. *The Throne of Broken Ruins* by Amber V. Nicole
* **Approximate Price:** $16.99

This dark, gothic fantasy features gods, monsters, and a deeply atmospheric world. It’s perfect for readers who enjoy the darker, folklore-heavy elements of fantasy, reminiscent of the swamps of Velen in *The Witcher 3*. It’s a story of vengeance, ancient curses, and morally grey characters who aren’t afraid to burn the world down.

### 9. *The Bound and the Broken: Of War and Ruin* by Ryan Cahill
* **Approximate Price:** $15.99

If you miss the epic scale of *A Song of Ice and Fire*, Ryan Cahill’s indie hit series is the closest thing you’ll find to classic high fantasy updated with modern, gritty sensibilities. It features dragons, massive battles, political betrayals, and a sprawling cast of characters that will keep you hooked for thousands of pages.

### 10. *The Darkness Within Us* by Jessica S. Olson
* **Approximate Price:** $16.99

A darker, gothic mystery that leans heavily into psychological horror. It tells the story of a girl who can see the darkness and sins within people's souls. When she is forced to use her powers for a corrupt royal court, she gets tangled in a web of murders and forbidden magic. It’s atmospheric, tense, and beautifully written.

---

## Bottom Line: Our Verdict

If you can only pick up two books from this list to satisfy your dark fantasy cravings in 2025, here is our verdict:

* **For the Action & Monster-Hunting Fan:** Go with **Jay Kristoff’s *Empire of the Damned***. Its relentless pacing, brutal combat, and atmospheric world are the closest literary equivalent to playing *The Witcher 3* on Death March difficulty.
* **For the Political & Mystery Fan:** Go with **Robert Jackson Bennett’s *The Tainted Cup***. It is a brilliant, fresh take on the fantasy genre that combines the best elements of a Sherlock Holmes mystery with terrifying, organic body-infused magic systems.

Whichever you choose, these books will easily keep you entertained while we wait for the next generation of dark fantasy gaming to arrive.
