## The Discovery: Frame-by-Frame Eagle-Eyed Gaming

The secret was revealed by a community member going by the handle 'RivellonScholar', who spent hours adjusting contrast, color spectrums, and playback speeds on cinematic trailers from *Divinity: Original Sin 2*. By running these runes through a translation cypher based on ancient Rivellonian script found in obscure in-game books, the community member translated a hidden sentence: *"When the Seven fall, the Voidwalker awakens beneath the blood-sea."*

While causal players might write this off as atmospheric fluff, seasoned *Divinity* historians know this specific phrasing does not match any current ending or lore document available in *Divinity: Original Sin 2*. It strongly hints at a dormant threat—or perhaps an unannounced continuation of the mainline *Divinity* storyline that Larian quietly planted years ago.
