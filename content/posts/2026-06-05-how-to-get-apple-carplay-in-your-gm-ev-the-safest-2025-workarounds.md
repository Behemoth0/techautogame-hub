## The Great GM EV Screen Lockout: Why Drivers Want CarPlay Back

When General Motors made the controversial decision to phase out Apple CarPlay and Android Auto in its new electric vehicle lineup—starting with the Chevrolet Blazer EV, Equinox EV, and Cadillac Lyriq—the automotive world reacted with collective disbelief. GM’s strategy was clear: push drivers toward their native, subscription-ready "Google built-in" infotainment system. 

However, in 2025, many drivers are finding that proprietary systems simply cannot replace the seamless, familiar, and crucially *safe* experience of Apple CarPlay. Stripping away CarPlay isn't just an inconvenience; it is a safety concern. Drivers rely on muscle memory to navigate their playlists, reply to messages via Siri, and use preferred navigation apps like Waze or Apple Maps. Forcing drivers to learn a new, unfamiliar interface while driving increases cognitive load and eyes-off-the-road time—the primary catalysts for distracted driving accidents.

Fortunately, tech manufacturers have stepped up. If you own or are planning to buy a GM-built EV, there are now highly effective, safe, and affordable ways to get Apple CarPlay back into your cockpit without voiding your vehicle's warranty.

---

## The Safety Case for Apple CarPlay in the EV Era

Before diving into the hardware, it is essential to understand why CarPlay is a critical safety system. According to the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA), distracted driving claimed over 3,300 lives in recent years. 

CarPlay mitigates this risk in three key ways:
1. **Siri Voice Integration:** Allowing drivers to dictate texts, make calls, and set navigation destinations without taking their hands off the steering wheel or eyes off the road.
2. **Simplified UI:** The large, high-contrast app grids are designed to be read in a fraction of a second, minimizing driver distraction.
3. **Consistency Across Vehicles:** If you switch between your GM EV and another family car, having the exact same interface reduces cognitive friction.

By utilizing aftermarket portable smart screens, you not only restore CarPlay, but you can also add vital Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS) and dashcam functionalities that your GM EV might not have come with.

---

## The Best Solutions to Get CarPlay in Your GM EV (2025 Edition)

Because GM has entirely blocked CarPlay at the software level on their native screens, standard OBD2 dongles or USB adapters will not work. The only reliable solution is a **portable dashboard smart screen**. These devices mount cleanly to your dash or windshield, connect wirelessly to your iPhone, and route audio directly through your GM EV's existing sound system via Bluetooth or AUX.

Here are the top-rated, safety-focused products available in 2025 to get the job done:

### 1. Carpuride W901 Pro (Dual Bluetooth & Built-in Dashcam)
* **Approximate Price:** $229.00
* **Why It’s Great for Safety:** The Carpuride W901 Pro is a premium 9-inch IPS display that features a built-in front-facing loop-recording camera. This effectively turns your CarPlay screen into an active dashcam, providing crucial video evidence in the event of an accident. 
* **The GM EV Fit:** It features "Dual Bluetooth" technology. This means your iPhone connects wirelessly to the Carpuride screen for CarPlay, while the screen simultaneously sends the audio output directly to your GM EV's Bluetooth system. You get CarPlay on a gorgeous screen, but the audio still plays through your car’s premium Bose speakers with zero lag.

### 2. Road Top 8.8-inch Ultra-Wide Portable Screen
* **Approximate Price:** $159.00
* **Why It’s Great for Safety:** One of the main safety concerns with dashboard screens is windshield obstruction. The Road Top 8.8-inch screen features a low-profile, ultra-wide aspect ratio. It sits low on your dashboard, preserving your forward field of view while still delivering a highly readable widescreen layout for maps and split-screen audio controls.
* **The GM EV Fit:** Its sleek design matches the futuristic, angular aesthetic of modern EV interiors like the Lyriq and Blazer EV, looking more like an OEM addition than an aftermarket hack.

### 3. Spedal CL860 2K Smart Dash Cam & CarPlay Screen (with ADAS)
* **Approximate Price:** $139.00
* **Why It’s Great for Safety:** This unit is a powerhouse for active safety. Beyond providing wireless Apple CarPlay, the Spedal CL860 features an integrated 2K dashcam equipped with basic ADAS (Advanced Driver Assistance Systems) features. It can provide visual and auditory warnings for lane departures and forward collision risks—a fantastic safety upgrade for base-trim GM EVs that may lack these premium active safety features.
* **The GM EV Fit:** A budget-friendly way to solve the CarPlay omission while adding an extra layer of road safety monitoring.

---

## How to Install Your CarPlay Screen Safely

To ensure your new screen doesn't become a hazard itself, follow these safety-first installation rules:
* **Avoid Airbag Deployment Zones:** Never mount the screen or run wires across the passenger-side dashboard or the A-pillars where side-curtain airbags deploy.
* **Keep Your View Clear:** Mount the screen as low as possible on the center dash. It should be easily viewable with a quick glance, but it should not block your view of the hood or the road ahead.
* **Secure Cable Management:** Use adhesive cable clips to tuck the power cable (usually running to the 12V cigarette lighter port or a USB-C port) behind the dash panels. Loose wires can interfere with your gear shifter, pedals, or hands on the wheel.
