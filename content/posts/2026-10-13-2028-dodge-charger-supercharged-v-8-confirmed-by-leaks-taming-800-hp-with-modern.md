## The Rumor Becomes Reality: A Supercharged V-8 Returns

Automotive enthusiasts let out a collective sigh of relief this week as leaked internal supplier documents confirmed what many prayed for: Dodge is officially preparing a supercharged V-8 powertrain for the Dodge Charger lineup, slated for the 2028 model year. While Stellantis initially leaned heavily into the twin-turbo inline-six 'Hurricane' engine and the all-electric Daytona platform, consumer demand for raw American displacement has forced their hand.

Yet, bringing back a high-octane monster with rumored outputs hovering between 800 and 900 horsepower poses a completely different challenge in today's regulatory landscape: automotive safety. In an era where the Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS) and the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) are tightening safety mandates, managing that much torque demands far more than rear-wheel burnouts and nostalgia. Modern performance engineering requires an obsessive focus on Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS), structural integrity, and cutting-edge collision avoidance.

## Taming the Beast: Muscle Car Dynamics vs. Modern ADAS

The previous generation of Hellcats gained a notorious reputation on public roads. Enormous low-end torque combined with antiquated chassis architecture often resulted in snap-oversteer accidents, wet-weather hydroplaning, and disproportionately high insurance loss data according to the Highway Loss Data Institute (HLDI). 

For the 2028 Dodge Charger to succeed, Stellantis must integrate safety technology that works in harmony with severe performance rather than choking it off. The STLA Large platform, which underpins the latest Charger generation, introduces high-speed compute modules capable of executing active safety interventions in milliseconds.

### Crucial Safety Systems for an 800-HP Platform:
- **Predictive Electronic Stability Control (ESC):** Unlike early traction control that abruptly cut fuel or dropped timing, next-gen systems use predictive yaw sensors and individual wheel torque vectoring to catch rotational slips before drivers even perceive the breakaway.
- **High-Speed Autonomous Emergency Braking (AEB):** Typical consumer AEB targets city speeds of 25 to 45 mph. A supercharged muscle car requires dual-camera and forward radar arrays capable of detecting obstacles and pedestrians at highway-plus closing speeds.
- **Adaptive Active Blind Spot Intervention:** Long hoods, wide haunches, and thick C-pillars are staple muscle car design cues that ruin sightlines. Integrating radar-assisted steering resistance when changing lanes into occupied zones is vital.

## Crashworthiness: Can the STLA Large Platform Handle the Impact?

Safety is not just software—it is physics. The 2028 Dodge Charger must satisfy the latest IIHS 'Top Safety Pick+' criteria, which became significantly tougher throughout 2024 and 2025. The updated side-impact test now utilizes a heavier, faster-moving barrier to replicate modern electric trucks, while the updated moderate front overlap test places rigorous scrutiny on rear-passenger chest and head deceleration.

Documents indicate the front subframe designed for the 2028 supercharged V-8 incorporates hydroformed high-strength steel crash rails specifically tailored to route kinetic impact around the heavy iron or aluminum block. This mitigates passenger compartment intrusion, a chronic issue in high-displacement muscle car collisions over the last twenty years.

## Essential Driver-Safety Accessories for High-Power Vehicles

If you plan to pilot a high-output vehicle like the upcoming supercharged Charger—or if you're driving a modern high-performance sports sedan in 2025—factory safety suites are only part of the equation. Equipping your vehicle with premium aftermarket safety, recording, and situational awareness hardware can dramatically reduce risk and protect your liability.

Here are four top-tier safety products built to keep high-speed drivers protected:

### 1. Escort MAXcam 360c ($849.99)
The Escort MAXcam 360c combines premium dual-antenna radar detection with a sharp 4K dash camera. For high-horsepower cars, situational awareness is safety. It features connected Wi-Fi database alerts for red-light cameras, speed zones, and road hazards, giving drivers ample reaction time. The seamless integration eliminates clutter on the windshield while providing loop-recorded proof of driving events.

### 2. Nextbase iQ 4K Smart Dash Cam ($599.99)
For active vehicle defense, the Nextbase iQ is one of the most advanced AI-powered dash cams on the market. It offers real-time spatial awareness, remote live-view over 4G LTE, and an 'Emergency SOS' feature that automatically contacts dispatchers with your GPS coordinates and medical details if a severe collision is detected. For a high-velocity Charger, this automated response system is an invaluable lifesaver.

### 3. VIOFO A229 Pro 3-Channel System ($359.99)
If you want complete 360-degree coverage without monthly cloud subscription fees, the A229 Pro utilizes dual Sony STARVIS 2 sensors covering the front, interior cabin, and rear window. Its interior IR sensor monitors driver awareness, while the high dynamic range (HDR) sensor ensures license plates and road obstacles are legible even under blinding headlights or pitch-black highways.

### 4. Garmin Catalyst Driving Performance Optimizer ($999.00)
For those taking their supercharged machinery to road courses or drag strips, driver error is the number one safety hazard. The Garmin Catalyst serves as a real-time virtual driving coach. Mounted to the windshield, it analyzes apex lines, braking points, and car balance, providing audio coaching directly through your headset to prevent catastrophic track-day spinouts.

## The Insurance Factor: Will Modern Safety Tame Hellcat Premiums?

One of the biggest hurdles facing the upcoming 2028 supercharged Charger will not be showroom floor demand—it will be insurance rates. Insurers regularly drop coverage or demand astronomical premiums for high-performance Dodges due to theft frequency and single-vehicle crash losses.

By packaging Level 2+ ADAS standard, integrating geofenced teen-driver or valet speed limiters, and incorporating advanced immobilizer protocols in the vehicle's electrical architecture, Dodge has an opportunity to rewrite the narrative. A supercharged muscle car does not have to be an insurance liability if modern automotive safety systems are embedded directly into its DNA.

## Bottom Line / Our Verdict

The return of a supercharged V-8 to the 2028 Dodge Charger proves that raw horsepower is not going quietly into the night. However, brute mechanical force alone is no longer enough to build an icon. To make this car a long-term triumph, Stellantis must back up its supercharged power with segment-leading active collision avoidance, robust chassis crashworthiness, and uncompromising occupant safety. Muscle without control is just a crash waiting to happen; muscle with cutting-edge ADAS is the future of American performance.
