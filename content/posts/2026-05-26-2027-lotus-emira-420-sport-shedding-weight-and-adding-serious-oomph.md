## Introduction: The Final ICE Swan Song Gets Louder

When Lotus first introduced the Emira, it was billed as the historic British marque’s final internal combustion engine (ICE) sports car. It was a bittersweet moment for automotive purists. We knew the end of an era was coming, but Lotus promised to make it memorable. Following the massive success of the 2025 Lotus Emira V6 and I4 models, Hethel has decided to give its mid-engine masterpiece one last, glorious evolution before the electric dawn fully takes over. 

Enter the **2027 Lotus Emira 420 Sport**. 

This isn't just a cosmetic package or a badge-engineering exercise. The 420 Sport represents the absolute pinnacle of Lotus’s traditional philosophy: "simplify, then add lightness," combined with a healthy dose of modern horsepower. By shedding crucial pounds and extracting more performance from its powertrain, the 2027 Emira 420 Sport bridges the gap between a daily-driver sports car and a hardcore track weapon.

## Under the Hood: Extracting the "420" Oomph

The heart of the 2027 Lotus Emira 420 Sport is a heavily revised version of the Mercedes-AMG sourced 2.0-liter turbocharged inline-four engine. While the 2025 base model put down a respectable 360 horsepower, Lotus engineers have worked closely with AMG to unlock the engine's true potential. 

Through a combination of a larger twin-scroll turbocharger, a redesigned carbon-fiber air intake, a freer-flowing titanium exhaust system, and a bespoke ECU remap, the 420 Sport now pumps out an eye-watering **420 horsepower** and 380 lb-ft of torque. Power is routed to the rear wheels via a lightning-fast 8-speed dual-clutch transmission (DCT) that has been recalibrated for more aggressive downshifts and shorter gear ratios.

For the purists who still demand three pedals, Lotus also offers a limited-run 420 Sport V6. Using the venerable Toyota-sourced 3.5-liter supercharged V6, Lotus has swapped the pulley and optimized the breathing to match the same 420-horsepower output, mated to a glorious six-speed manual gearbox with exposed gear-shift linkage.

0-60 mph times drop to a blistering **3.5 seconds** for the DCT model, while top speed climbs to 186 mph. This isn't just fast for a Lotus; it's supercar territory.

## The Diet: How Lotus Shed the Weight

Power is only half of the Lotus equation. To truly deserve the "Sport" moniker, the Emira had to go on a strict diet. The standard Emira, while dynamic, was criticized by some hardcore brand loyalists as being a bit too heavy, tipping the scales at over 3,100 lbs due to modern safety standards and luxury creature comforts.

The 2027 Emira 420 Sport slashes nearly **115 pounds** off the curb weight through several targeted engineering changes:

*   **Carbon Fiber Body Panels:** The hood, roof panel, and active rear spoiler are now constructed from high-grade, exposed carbon fiber.
*   **Forged Ultra-Lightweight Wheels:** New 20-inch forged alloy wheels save a collective 18 pounds of unsprung weight.
*   **Titanium Exhaust:** The lightweight titanium exhaust system not only sounds like a miniature GT3 race car but also cuts 15 pounds from the rear overhang.
*   **Interior Stripping:** Out go the heavy 12-way power-adjustable leather seats, replaced by manual-adjust carbon-fiber bucket seats wrapped in Alcantara. Sound deadening has also been strategically reduced in non-critical areas, allowing more of that mechanical symphony to filter into the cabin.

These weight savings completely transform the car's rotational inertia, making the turn-in feel sharper, more immediate, and incredibly communicative.

## Track-Ready Upgrades: Enhancing the Drive

To complement the added power and reduced mass, Lotus has overhauled the chassis dynamics. The 420 Sport features a stiffer, track-focused suspension setup utilizing adjustable Bilstein dampers and Eibach anti-roll bars. 

Aerodynamics have also been subtly but effectively revised. A new front splitter and a redesigned rear diffuser work in tandem with the active carbon spoiler to increase downforce by 12% at high speeds, ensuring the car remains glued to the tarmac during high-speed sweepers.

## Bottom Line / Our Verdict

The 2027 Lotus Emira 420 Sport is exactly the car that driving enthusiasts have been begging for. While the 2025 Emira was a gorgeous, highly capable daily-drivable sports car, it occasionally felt a bit too polished, hiding some of the raw, visceral feedback that made legendary cars like the Elise and Exige famous. 

By shedding weight, sharpening the chassis, and boosting output to 420 horsepower, Lotus has injected a healthy dose of analog soul back into this digital age. It represents a flawless send-off to the internal combustion engine. If you have the means, buy one, cherish it, and take it to the track. Cars like this will never be made again.
