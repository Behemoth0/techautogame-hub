## The Best Tech & Accessories to Upgrade Your Setup

### 1. Sony PlayStation 5 Pro
* **Approximate Price:** $699

If the June 2026 State of Play proved anything, it is that standard hardware is being pushed to its absolute limits. To enjoy native 4K resolution at a locked 60 frames per second (or even up to 120 FPS in performance modes), the **PlayStation 5 Pro** is an essential investment. Released as the mid-generation pinnacle of Sony's hardware lineup, the PS5 Pro utilizes advanced AI-driven upscaling (PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution) and a significantly beefier GPU. During the showcase, games like the new *Horizon* project demonstrated dramatically improved ray-traced reflections and far more stable framerates on the Pro model compared to the base console. It is the definitive way to future-proof your living room.

### 2. WD_BLACK SN850X 2TB NVMe SSD (with Heatsink)
* **Approximate Price:** $159

With 2026's blockbuster titles boasting incredibly complex textures and massive open worlds, game install sizes are ballooning past the 150GB mark. The standard internal storage of your console will fill up after installing just a handful of titles. The **WD_BLACK SN850X 2TB NVMe SSD** is the gold standard for PlayStation storage expansion. Boasting blistering read speeds of up to 7,300 MB/s, this drive matches and often exceeds the performance of Sony's internal SSD. The pre-installed heatsink ensures that even during marathon gaming sessions in hot summer months, your drive remains cool and free from thermal throttling.

### 3. SteelSeries Arctis Nova Pro Wireless for PlayStation
* **Approximate Price:** $349

Sound design is half of the gaming experience, especially with Sony’s Tempest 3D AudioTech being heavily utilized in the newly announced psychological thrillers. The **SteelSeries Arctis Nova Pro Wireless** is widely regarded as the ultimate gaming headset. It features a dual-battery hot-swap system, meaning you never have to plug your headset in to charge mid-game. With Active Noise Cancellation (ANC), premium high-fidelity audio drivers, and a wireless base station that lets you adjust EQ settings on the fly, this headset ensures you will hear every creeping footstep and distant explosion with pinpoint spatial accuracy.

### 4. PlayStation VR2
* **Approximate Price:** $549

Virtual reality took center stage during the mid-section of the June 2026 State of Play. With three major AAA VR titles announced, there has never been a better time to jump into the ecosystem. The **PlayStation VR2** headset features a stunning OLED display with 2000 x 2040 resolution per eye, smooth 90Hz/120Hz refresh rates, and incredibly immersive headset feedback. Combined with the Sense controllers' adaptive triggers and precise haptics, the PS VR2 turns gaming from a passive viewing experience into an incredibly tactile, physical adventure.

### 5. Sony DualSense Edge Wireless Controller
* **Approximate Price:** $199

For competitive multiplayer reveals and high-octane action games showcased during the event, standard controllers just do not offer enough customization. The **DualSense Edge** is Sony's answer to professional-grade gaming. It features remappable buttons, swappable stick caps, adjustable trigger stops, and replaceable stick modules (saving you from buying a whole new controller if stick drift ever occurs). The back paddles are a game-changer for complex control schemes, allowing you to jump, slide, or reload without ever taking your thumbs off the analog sticks.
