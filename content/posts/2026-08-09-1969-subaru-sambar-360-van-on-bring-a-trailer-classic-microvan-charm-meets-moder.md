In an era dominated by 5,000-pound electric SUVs and high-speed highway traffic, driving an unprotected vintage van requires a proactive approach to safety. ## Fire Safety and Emergency Mitigation for Rear-Engined Classics

### 5. Element E50 Portable Fire Extinguisher
*Approximate Price: $80*

The **Element E50** uses a solid potassium gas discharge to smother fires without leaving any messy residue. It is 80% smaller than a conventional extinguisher, never expires, and easily mounts under the Sambar’s bench seat or dashboard for immediate emergency access.

## Essential Checklist Before Driving Your Bring a Trailer Sambar

If you win the auction on Bring a Trailer, follow this critical safety prep checklist before your first neighborhood drive:

* **Seatbelt Retrofit:** Upgrade original lap belts to modern three-point retractable seatbelts with reinforced floor anchors.
* **Braking System Overhaul:** Rebuild the single-circuit master cylinder, replace rubber brake lines with stainless braided lines, and inspect drum shoes.
* **Tire Replacement:** Antique 10-inch bias-ply tires lack grip. Switch to modern radial tires designed for classic mini trucks to improve wet-weather braking distance.
* **Horn Upgrade:** The factory single-tone horn is easily drowned out by modern road noise. Install a compact high-output horn so larger vehicles know you are there.
