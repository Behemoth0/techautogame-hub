## A Tragic Setback and an Unprecedented Solution

For Polish gamers, Jacek Rozenek is not merely a performer—he is the definitive voice of Geralt of Rivia. Long before Doug Cockle brought the White Wolf to life for English-speaking audiences, Rozenek defined the stoic, world-weary persona of Andrzej Sapkowski's monster slayer across the original *Witcher* trilogy. However, in May 2019, tragedy struck when Rozenek suffered an extensive ischemic stroke. The medical emergency left him partially paralyzed and caused severe speech impairment, casting serious doubt on whether he would ever step inside a recording booth again.

Yet, behind closed doors at CD Projekt Red, audio engineers and narrative designers were working on a quiet, unprecedented rescue mission. Instead of replacing the beloved veteran actor, the studio partnered with cutting-edge AI vocal restoration firms to modify and repair Rozenek’s recorded lines. By training an algorithmic voice model on his extensive pre-stroke catalogue, CD Projekt was able to blend his current physical performance with his historic vocal timbre, returning the legendary monster hunter to his familiar, gravelly glory.

CD Projekt leadership was quick to emphasize the unique context of the initiative: "This was a special case." As the gaming landscape in 2025 grapples fiercely with the ethical dilemmas of generative AI and voice actor strikes, this moving restoration reveals what technological empathy looks like in practice.

## 'A Special Case': Ethics, Consent, and Restorative AI

Over the past two years, artificial intelligence in the creative sector has faced well-earned skepticism. From SAG-AFTRA strikes protesting uncompensated digital likenesses to indie developers using automated synthetic voiceovers to undercut freelance performers, the gaming industry has felt the friction of automation. 

What makes CD Projekt’s approach with Jacek Rozenek fundamentally distinct is the central tenets of actor agency, informed consent, and active participation. Rozenek was not circumvented or replaced; he was seated in the recording studio delivering every single line himself. His raw emotional cadence, dramatic timing, and veteran acting choices were intact, but his vocal cords—physically scarred by the stroke—lacked the lower-register power and fluidity of Geralt's signature tone.

Using proprietary resynthesis techniques, engineers mapped his historic vocal profile directly onto his new live recordings. The studio worked directly alongside Rozenek and his family throughout the entire post-production pipeline, ensuring he was compensated as the primary voice talent. By framing this specifically as a medical accommodation and restorative tribute rather than a cost-cutting shortcut, CD Projekt has drawn a stark, commendable line in the sand regarding how generative audio tools ought to be deployed.

## The Technological Breakthrough Behind Voice Reconstruction

Reconstructing an organic human voice goes far beyond basic text-to-speech generators. Human speech contains microscopic inflections, subtle dynamic breath pauses, regional vowel colorings, and deep sub-harmonic frequencies that traditional parametric filters flatten entirely.

To achieve audio fidelity worthy of a flagship RPG, the sound design crew utilized deep neural networks (DNNs) focused on timbre conversion. The model analyzes Rozenek's current phonemes in real time and applies the acoustic envelope of his 2015 performance during *The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt*. The result avoids the uncanny valley effect that plagues synthetic speech, preserving the human soul of the performance while repairing the physical limitations imposed by illness.

## The Bigger Picture: AI Voice Acting vs. Human Preservation

CD Projekt’s compassionate collaboration with Jacek Rozenek provides a vital counter-argument to the prevailing narrative that AI in creative fields is inherently exploitative. When deployed with complete transparency, living artist authorization, and fair compensation, machine learning can serve as an assistive prosthetic rather than a hostile replacement.

Rozenek himself has publicly expressed deep gratitude for the opportunity to retain his defining artistic identity despite profound health struggles. In an era where corporate bottom lines routinely eclipse human considerations, this project serves as a reminder that technology is at its best when it lifts up human artists who have given decades of passion to the medium.

For gamers, it means getting to hear a legend continue his journey; for the wider tech world, it establishes a compelling blueprint for ethical AI development in 2025 and beyond.
