## Introduction: The Next-Gen Nintendo Era Has Arrived

Following the massive excitement of Nintendo's late 2025 hardware transition, the Nintendo Switch 2 (officially dubbed by fans and insiders alike) has firmly established itself as the must-have gaming console of the decade. Boasting custom NVIDIA Ampere architecture, DLSS upscaling, and a gorgeous 8-inch display, the console has finally given developers the processing muscle they have craved for years. 

Now that we are in June 2026, the second wave of software releases is hitting store shelves and the Nintendo eShop. This month is particularly packed with system-sellers, highly anticipated sequels, and third-party powerhouses that show off what this hybrid machine can truly do. 

If you are looking to expand your digital library this summer, here are the 6 best new Switch 2 games you need to play in June 2026.

---

## 1. Super Mario Odyssey 2

* **Approximate Price:** $69.99
* **Developer/Publisher:** Nintendo
* **Genre:** 3D Platformer

It has been nearly a decade since Mario and Cappy redefined the 3D platforming genre. In *Super Mario Odyssey 2*, Nintendo takes full advantage of the Switch 2's upgraded hardware to deliver sprawling, seamless sandbox kingdoms without a single loading screen. 

This time around, Mario travels to cosmic dimensions and sub-atomic realms, utilizing a new gravity-shifting mechanic that works in tandem with Cappy's traditional capture abilities. The visual upgrade is immediately apparent: running at a locked 1080p in handheld mode and a stunning, DLSS-reconstructed 4K at 60fps on your living room television, the game features realistic fabric textures on Mario's overalls and breathtaking volumetric lighting in the new Neon Metropolis kingdom. It is the quintessential showcase for your new hardware.

## 2. Metroid Prime 4: Beyond (Switch 2 Enhanced Edition)

* **Approximate Price:** $69.99
* **Developer/Publisher:** Retro Studios / Nintendo
* **Genre:** First-Person Adventure / Sci-Fi

After one of the most famous development cycles in gaming history, Samus Aran's next grand adventure is finally here, and it was worth the wait. While a standard version exists for legacy hardware, the *Switch 2 Enhanced Edition* is the definitive way to experience the dark, atmospheric corridors of the planet Elysia Prime.

Retro Studios has utilized the Switch 2's ray-tracing capabilities to create hyper-realistic reflections on Samus's visor and dynamic, real-time shadows cast by her arm cannon's plasma blasts. The gameplay is tighter than ever, offering dual-analog precision controls and gyro-aiming that feels incredibly fluid. If you want a mature, cinematic, and graphically jaw-dropping experience this June, this is your game.

## 3. Xenoblade Chronicles 4: Dawn of Destiny

* **Approximate Price:** $69.99
* **Developer/Publisher:** Monolith Soft / Nintendo
* **Genre:** Open-World JRPG

Monolith Soft is legendary for squeezing every ounce of power out of Nintendo hardware, and *Xenoblade Chronicles 4: Dawn of Destiny* is a technical marvel. Set in a brand-new universe separate from the Klaus saga, *Dawn of Destiny* introduces players to a world where floating continents are slowly colliding.

Thanks to the Switch 2's custom NVMe SSD speeds, fast-traveling across the massive, multi-tiered world map takes less than two seconds. The game features a revised real-time combat system with seamless party transitions. Visually, the anime-inspired art style is elevated by clean anti-aliasing and a draw distance that stretches infinitely into the horizon, making exploration a pure joy.

## 4. Mario Kart 9: Crossroads

* **Approximate Price:** $69.99
* **Developer/Publisher:** Nintendo
* **Genre:** Arcade Racer

*Mario Kart 8 Deluxe* is officially a relic of the past. *Mario Kart 9: Crossroads* reinvents the franchise by introducing a fully realized adventure mode and cross-franchise tracks that blur the lines between Nintendo properties. Drive through the post-apocalyptic ruins of Hyrule or dodge traffic in Animal Crossing's bustling plaza.

On the Switch 2, *Crossroads* supports chaotic 12-player local split-screen (when docked to multiple screens via local wireless) and an ultra-smooth 120Hz refresh rate mode for compatible gaming monitors. The sense of speed is unmatched, and the updated physics engine makes drifting feel more responsive than ever before.

## 5. Monster Hunter Wilds: Portable Edition

* **Approximate Price:** $59.99
* **Developer/Publisher:** Capcom
* **Genre:** Action RPG / Multiplayer

Capcom continues its excellent support for Nintendo hardware with a specially optimized port of *Monster Hunter Wilds*. Dubbed the *Portable Edition*, this version is tailor-made for the Switch 2's architecture.

While it scales back some of the ultra-high-end PC settings, the clever use of NVIDIA DLSS ensures that the game runs at a stable 45–60fps even during chaotic hunts involving giant monsters and dynamic weather storms. The game features full cross-play with PC and home consoles, allowing you to hunt with your friends on the go. It is a massive, hundreds-of-hours-long time sink that justifies the purchase of the console on its own.

## 6. Hollow Knight: Silksong (Physical Deluxe Edition)

* **Approximate Price:** $39.99
* **Developer/Publisher:** Team Cherry / Fangamer
* **Genre:** Metroidvania

Yes, it is real, and yes, it is spectacular. While *Silksong* launched digitally to critical acclaim, June 2026 marks the arrival of the highly coveted Physical Deluxe Edition, published in partnership with Fangamer. 

Playing as Hornet through the kingdom of Pharloom feels incredibly crisp on the Switch 2's high-contrast screen. The hand-drawn 2D art style pops with vibrant colors, and the console's improved haptic feedback triggers rumble subtly when you parry an enemy attack or heal using silk. For fans of challenging platformers, this physical release is a gorgeous addition to any shelf.

## Hardware Spotlight: Why the Switch 2 Changes the Game

What makes these June 2026 releases feel so different from the games of the previous generation is the sheer stability of the performance. The original Switch often forced developers to choose between resolution and frame rate, leading to blurry textures and choppy gameplay in ambitious titles. 

With the Switch 2, the integration of modern upscaling technologies means we are getting the best of both worlds: crisp, high-definition visuals on the go and near-PS5-level performance when docked. The improved battery life—averaging 4 to 6 hours even during intensive play sessions of *Super Mario Odyssey 2*—makes it the ultimate travel companion for the summer.

---

## Bottom Line / Our Verdict

If you have been waiting for the right moment to dive headfirst into the next generation of Nintendo gaming, June 2026 is the perfect time to do so. The initial launch library of late 2025 laid a solid foundation, but this current wave of software truly demonstrates the generational leap.

If you can only buy two games this month, we highly recommend pairing the unparalleled platforming joy of **Super Mario Odyssey 2** with the deep, action-packed multiplayer of **Monster Hunter Wilds: Portable Edition**. Together, they represent the absolute pinnacle of what the Nintendo Switch 2 has to offer: unmatched first-party magic and uncompromising third-party portability.
