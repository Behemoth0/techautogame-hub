## The Ultimate Test of Patience in Gielinor

Old School RuneScape (OSRS) is famously known for its grueling end-game grinds, restricted account builds, and a community willing to put thousands of hours into absurd self-imposed challenges. But just when you think you have seen every possible variation of snowflake ironman or region-locked hero, someone pushes the boundaries of human endurance even further.

Recently, an OSRS player accomplished what many considered an exercise in sheer existential madness: reaching Level 99 Fishing exclusively using the Small Fishing Net—the very first tool handed to players on Tutorial Island. When asked by fellow community members why anyone would subject themselves to hundreds of hours of catching low-level raw shrimp and anchovies, the player succinctly summarized the entire philosophy of Old School RuneScape with a simple three-word phrase: “Nothing matters.”

In 2025, as MMORPGs increasingly pivot toward rapid progression, automated questing, and instant gratification, OSRS stands as a monument to slow, deliberate grind culture. This incredible feat is a testament to the unyielding spirit of PC gamers who treat tedious repetition not as a chore, but as an art form.

## Millions of Shrimp: Calculating the Net-Only Grind

To understand how unhinged this achievement truly is, you have to look at the numbers. Reaching Level 99 in any OSRS skill requires a total of 13,034,431 experience points. Under normal circumstances, players transition from starter nets to fly fishing rods, harpoons, barb fishing, or the Tempoross minigame to maximize XP rates—often exceeding 70,000 to 100,000 XP per hour at higher levels.

By restricting themselves strictly to the Small Fishing Net, this player capped their experience rates at a fraction of standard methods. Catching raw shrimp grants a modest 10 XP per catch, while raw anchovies grant 40 XP. At an average rate of roughly 15,000 to 20,000 XP per hour, reaching Level 99 requires roughly 700 to 900 hours of continuous, active clicking at basic fishing spots around Lumbridge, Al Kharid, and Catherby.

That translates to catching over 400,000 individual fish. It represents months of real-world time dedicated to clicking tiny blue fishing spots on a screen, dropping inventory after inventory, all for a digital skillcape and ultimate flexing rights on the Reddit forums.

## Why Restricted OSRS Builds Rule the Gaming Scene in 2025

Why do players do this? Self-imposed restrictions have become the lifeblood of OSRS stream culture and YouTube content creation in 2025. From Settled's legendary Swampletics series to single-chunk ironmen, restricting game mechanics forces players to re-examine nostalgia-filled worlds through an entirely new lens.

When standard gameplay loop achievements lose their novelty, creating ultra-niche goals provides a fresh dose of dopamine. In a world oversaturated with hyper-competitive Battle Royales and microtransaction-filled games, the humble Small Fishing Net grind offers something oddly meditative. It’s peak AFK (Away From Keyboard) gaming—allowing players to watch movies, study, or work remotely while slowly edging toward an impossible goal.

## Community Reactions: Existential Dread Meets Pure Awe

The OSRS community's reaction to the starter net accomplishment ranged from overwhelming admiration to humorous concern. Reddit threads exploded with memes, with users calculating the market value of 400,000 raw anchovies and debating the mental fortitude required to reject efficient training methods.

Whether you spend your time chasing high-tier Raids gear or catching low-level shrimp with a beginner net, the joy comes from setting a goal and seeing it through to the end.

## Мільйони креветок: підрахунки грінду лише сіткою

Щоб зрозуміти, наскільки безумним є це досягнення, варто поглянути на цифри. Досягнення 99-го рівня у будь-якій навичці OSRS вимагає загалом 13 034 431 очок досвіду (XP). За звичайних обставин гравці переходять від стартових сіток до вудок для лову на муху, гарпунів, барбаріанського рибальства чи мінігри Tempoross, щоб максимізувати швидкість отримання досвіду — нерідко перевищуючи від 70 000 до 100 000 XP на годину на вищих рівнях.

Обмеживши себе виключно маленькою рибальською сіткою, цей гравець зменшив свою швидкість набору досвіду до мізерної частки від стандартних методів. Це означає вилов понад 400 000 окремих рибин. Це місяці реального часу, присвячені кліканню по крихітних синіх рибальських точках на екрані та постійному викиданню інвентарю — і все це заради цифрового плаща навички (skillcape) та найвищого права похизуватися на форумах Reddit.
