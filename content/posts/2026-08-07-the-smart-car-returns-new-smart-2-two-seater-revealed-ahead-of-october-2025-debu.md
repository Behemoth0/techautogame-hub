## Introduction: The Iconic Microcar Reimagined

For city dwellers and microcar enthusiasts who feared the iconic two-seater ultra-compact vehicle was extinct, good news has arrived. Smart—the brand born from an improbable partnership between Swatch and Mercedes-Benz, and now revitalized as a high-tech joint venture between Mercedes-Benz Group and Geely—has officially teased its newest model: the Smart #2.

Scheduled for a full global debut this October ahead of its market rollout in late 2025, the Smart #2 marks a triumphant return to the brand’s original core identity. After launching larger crossover models like the Smart #1 and Smart #3, the automaker is finally returning to its roots with an agile, hyper-efficient two-seater engineered specifically for dense urban environments.

## What We Know About the New Smart #2

When Smart phased out the Fortwo EQ in early 2024, many worried that the spirit of the ultra-compact city car was lost to the industry's obsession with massive SUVs. However, official details ahead of the October reveal confirm that the Smart #2 is built from the ground up to redefine modern urban mobility.

Unlike its predecessors, the Smart #2 leverages Geely’s highly adaptable Sustainable Experience Architecture (SEA-E) platform, custom-tailored for small footprints. Visually, early renders and camouflage teasers showcase Mercedes-Benz’s modern Sensual Purity design language: smooth lines, ultra-slim LED light bars across the front and rear, and a distinctive two-tone floating roofline that pays homage to the original Tridion safety cell.

Despite its compact footprint—expected to measure roughly 2.75 meters (around 9 feet) in overall length—the Smart #2 utilizes smart EV packaging to maximize cabin room. By eliminating the engine bay entirely and pushing the wheels to the absolute corners of the frame, interior legroom and headroom rival that of a midsize sedan.

## Driving Dynamics and Range Expectation

While complete technical specifications will remain under wraps until the official press event in October, insider reports suggest the Smart #2 will feature a rear-mounted single electric motor pushing out roughly 110 to 130 horsepower. For a car weighing significantly less than traditional EVs, that power promises snappy 0-30 mph acceleration perfectly suited for darting through traffic light stops.

Battery technology has come a long way since the original Fortwo EV. The Smart #2 is expected to house a compact 35 kWh to 40 kWh LFP (Lithium Iron Phosphate) battery pack, delivering a real-world range of approximately 160 to 180 miles (WLTP). Crucially, 800V architecture support—or at minimum high-speed DC fast charging up to 100 kW—will allow drivers to top up from 10% to 80% in just under 20 minutes.

## The Competitive Landscape: Top Urban EVs Compared

To understand where the incoming Smart #2 fits into the 2025 EV market, it helps to look at current compact electric offerings. While many brands have abandoned tiny cars, a few standout urban models exist for buyers seeking efficient city transport right now.

### 1. Smart #1
- **Starting Price:** ~$38,000 (€37,000)
- **Overview:** The current entry point for Smart's modern era. A subcompact crossover offering 268 horsepower, premium interior materials, and a range of up to 273 miles. While far larger than the original Fortwo, it laid the technological groundwork for the incoming #2.

### 2. Mini Cooper Electric (2025 SE)
- **Starting Price:** ~$30,900 (€34,000)
- **Overview:** The classic British icon delivers punchy go-kart handling, a stunning round OLED touchscreen, and 215 horsepower. It stands as one of Smart #2’s primary lifestyle competitors, though it features a significantly larger footprint.

### 3. Fiat 500e
- **Starting Price:** ~$32,500 (€29,000)
- **Overview:** Stylish, Italian, and nimble, the Fiat 500e is currently one of the closest direct rivals to the Smart ethos. Offering 117 horsepower and an EPA-estimated range of 149 miles, it excels in tight municipal parking environments.

### 4. Volvo EX30
- **Starting Price:** ~$34,950 (€36,000)
- **Overview:** Built on a sibling variant of Geely’s SEA platform, the EX30 proves how much quality and safety technology can be crammed into a compact frame. It offers blazing performance and scandinavia-minimalist interior design.

### 5. Upcoming Smart #2 (Estimated Specs)
- **Estimated Starting Price:** ~$25,000 - $28,000 (€23,000 - €25,000)
- **Target Market:** Dedicated urbanites needing zero-emissions transport, effortless parallel parking capabilities, and modern smart-connectivity.

## Next-Gen Interior and Tech Innovations

Inside, the Smart #2 is set to showcase a radical departure from cheap plastics of the past. Mercedes-Benz designers have prioritized sustainable, high-touch materials, including recycled ocean plastics and vegan leather accents.

Tech infrastructure will be powered by SmartOS, running on a crisp 12.8-inch central display paired with an AI-assisted voice avatar. Features expected at reveal include:

- **Vehicle-to-Load (V2L) Capability:** Power small appliances or electronics directly from the car's charge port.
- **Over-The-Air (OTA) Updates:** Continuous software enhancements for drive modes, infotainment, and battery management.
- **Automated Park Assist:** Advanced sensor suites capable of maneuvering the car into spaces barely larger than the vehicle itself.

## Our Verdict: The Right Car at the Right Time

As modern electric cars continue to grow heavier, wider, and increasingly expensive, the reveal of the Smart #2 represents a refreshing return to sanity for urban mobility. By combining Geely’s efficient EV architecture with Mercedes-Benz design flair, Smart is poised to reclaim its title as the undisputed king of city driving.

If Smart can lock in an accessible starting price near $25,000 when sales commence following the October debut, the Smart #2 will likely be one of the most exciting and practical EV launches of 2025.
