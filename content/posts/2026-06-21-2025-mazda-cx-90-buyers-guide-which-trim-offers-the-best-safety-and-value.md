## Introduction: The Premium Family Hauler

Mazda has made no secret of its desire to move upmarket, and the 2025 Mazda CX-90 is the crown jewel of this ambitious transition. Replacing the beloved CX-9, this three-row flagship SUV features a brand-new rear-wheel-drive platform, a silky-smooth turbocharged inline-six engine, and an available plug-in hybrid (PHEV) powertrain. 

But for family buyers, luxury and performance take a back seat to one crucial factor: safety. Fortunately, Mazda has packed the CX-90 with its latest i-Activsense suite of Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS). With multiple trims spread across different price points, choosing the right model can feel overwhelming. In this guide, we break down the best 2025 Mazda CX-90 trims, analyzing their safety tech, features, and overall value to help you make the smartest buying decision.

## Safety First: IIHS Ratings and the i-Activsense Suite

Before diving into individual trims, let's look at the CX-90's safety foundation. The Mazda CX-90 earned the prestigious **Top Safety Pick+** award from the Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS). It achieved the highest rating of "Good" in all crashworthiness tests, including the updated, more stringent side impact and moderate front overlap tests.

Mazda’s standard safety suite, **i-Activsense**, is remarkably robust even on base models. Standard features across all trims include:
*   **Smart Brake Support (SBS):** Automatic emergency braking with pedestrian and cyclist detection.
*   **Blind Spot Monitoring (BSM):** Alerts you to vehicles in your blind spots.
*   **Lane-keep Assist System (LAS):** Gently steers the vehicle back into its lane if you drift.
*   **Rear Cross Traffic Alert (RCTA):** Warns of oncoming traffic when backing out of parking spots.
*   **Safe Exit Assist:** Prevents passengers from opening doors into oncoming traffic or cyclists.

However, if you want advanced active safety tech like hands-on traffic jam assist, 360-degree cameras, or front cross-traffic alerts, you will need to climb the trim ladder.

## The Contenders: 3 Best-Value Mazda CX-90 Trims Analyzed

To help you decide, we have selected three standout trims representing different budgets, powertrains, and safety priorities.

### 1. Mazda CX-90 3.3 Turbo Select (The Budget-Conscious Baseline)
*   **Approximate Price:** $39,300
*   **Best For:** Families looking for top-tier crash safety on a budget.

The **3.3 Turbo Select** serves as the entry point to the CX-90 lineup. Powered by a 280-horsepower 3.3-liter turbocharged inline-six engine, it offers standard all-wheel drive (AWD) and an impressive array of safety features. 

Despite being the base model, it does not skimp on structural safety or essential ADAS. You get the full standard i-Activsense suite, including Mazda Radar Cruise Control with Stop & Go. The primary drawback is the lack of convenience-focused safety tech, such as a 360-degree monitor or parking sensors, which can make parking this large SUV a bit challenging in tight spaces. However, at under $40,000, it offers unmatched structural safety for the price.

### 2. Mazda CX-90 3.3 Turbo Premium Plus (The Sweet Spot for Advanced Safety)
*   **Approximate Price:** $50,400
*   **Best For:** Tech-focused drivers who want maximum active safety and premium comfort.

If you want the absolute best ADAS technology Mazda has to offer without crossing into the ultra-luxury price bracket, the **3.3 Turbo Premium Plus** is the undisputed sweet spot. 

This trim adds highly sophisticated active safety upgrades that drastically reduce driver fatigue and prevent low-speed accidents:
*   **Cruising & Traffic Support (CTS):** Mazda's Level 2 driver-assist system that combines adaptive cruise control with lane-centering steering assist to manage stop-and-go highway traffic.
*   **360° View Monitor with See-Through View:** Uses cameras to project a virtual image of the area directly in front of and around the vehicle, eliminating blind spots when parking.
*   **Smart Brake Support Reverse (SBS-R):** Automatically applies the brakes if an obstacle or pedestrian is detected behind the vehicle while reversing.
*   **Front Cross Traffic Alert (FCTA) with Brake Support:** Warns you of vehicles approaching from the sides at blind intersections.

Combined with a panoramic sunroof, Nappa leather seating, and a larger 12.3-inch infotainment display, this trim delivers a true luxury experience with class-leading safety.

### 3. Mazda CX-90 PHEV Premium (The Eco-Friendly Safety Pick)
*   **Approximate Price:** $54,900
*   **Best For:** Eco-conscious commuters who want plug-in efficiency and robust safety.

For those looking to minimize fuel costs, the **PHEV Premium** offers a plug-in hybrid powertrain delivering 323 horsepower and up to 26 miles of pure electric driving range. 

On the safety front, the PHEV Premium bridges the gap between the mid-tier and top-tier trims. It features standard Cruising & Traffic Support (CTS) and blind-spot assist. While it lacks the "See-Through View" 360-degree camera found on the Premium Plus, it still features front and rear parking sensors and emergency lane-keeping assist, making it a highly secure and incredibly efficient family cruiser.

## Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS) Breakdown

To understand why we recommend upgrading to trims with the advanced i-Activsense package, let’s look at how these systems perform in real-world scenarios:

*   **Cruising & Traffic Support (CTS):** On long road trips or daily commutes, maintaining lane position and distance can be exhausting. CTS acts as a co-pilot, keeping you centered in your lane and managing speed smoothly, reducing driver fatigue.
*   **Smart Brake Support (SBS) Evolution:** Mazda's latest SBS system is incredibly precise. Unlike older systems that suffered from frequent false-positive alerts, the CX-90 utilizes advanced radar and camera fusion to accurately identify pedestrians, cyclists, and oncoming turn-across-path hazards.
*   **See-Through View Monitor:** Large SUVs are notorious for front-end blind spots. This system digitally "erases" the hood on your screen, allowing you to see curbs, toys, or small pets directly in front of the bumper.

## Bottom Line: Our Verdict

While the base **3.3 Turbo Select ($39,300)** is an incredible value that doesn't compromise on structural crash protection, it lacks the advanced parking and highway assist systems that make driving a large SUV stress-free.

For most families, the **Mazda CX-90 3.3 Turbo Premium Plus ($50,400)** is the **Best Buy**. It successfully bridges the gap between mainstream pricing and luxury-tier safety execution. With the inclusion of the 360-degree See-Through View monitor, Cruising & Traffic Support, and rear emergency braking, it provides maximum peace of mind, making it the smartest investment for your family's daily travels in 2025.
