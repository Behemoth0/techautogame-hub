## The Iron Fist of Doom: Reprinting "Toxic Deluge"

For years, black-aligned decks in the Commander format have relied on a select few board wipes to clear the path to victory. Chief among them is **Toxic Deluge**, a sorcery that allows you to pay an X amount of life to give all creatures -X/-X until end of turn. It bypasses indestructible, ignores ward, and can be cast for a mere three mana (one black, two colorless). 

Historically, *Toxic Deluge* has sat comfortably at a $15 to $22 price point depending on the printing. It is an absolute staple of the format, meaning demand is always sky-high. 

In the upcoming Doctor Doom product, *Toxic Deluge* is getting a glorious, villainous face-lift. The new artwork depicts Doctor Doom standing triumphantly over a defeated field of heroes and mechanical sentinels, surrounded by a corrosive, emerald-green chemical fog. The flavor text perfectly encapsulates Doom’s arrogant supremacy. For players who love to build thematic decks, this is the ultimate version of an already perfect card.

## Why This Reprint Matters for the MTG Economy in 2025

Whenever Wizards of the Coast reprints a highly sought-after staple in a specialized product, it has a fascinating ripple effect on the secondary market:

1. **Accessibility for Casual Players:** A $20 card can be a barrier to entry for budget-conscious players. By bundling this reprint in a highly anticipated Marvel drop, it injects more copies into the wild, temporarily lowering the barrier to entry.
2. **The "Pimp" Factor:** Collectors love unique art. The Marvel-themed *Toxic Deluge* is expected to carry a premium price tag on the secondary market, especially the foil and extended-art variants. 
3. **Cross-Pollination of Fandoms:** This card acts as a bridge. Marvel fans who have never played MTG are drawn in by the iconic artwork, while veteran Magic players get to upgrade their decks with pop-culture royalty.

## How to Play the New Doctor Doom in Commander

If you plan to build a deck around the new Doctor Doom card, you’ll be playing in the **Esper (White/Blue/Black)** or **Grixis (Blue/Black/Red)** color identities, depending on his final printed color identity. Doom’s mechanics heavily emphasize artifact synergy, reflecting his army of Doombots and his mastery of both magic and technology. 

Integrating the new *Toxic Deluge* into his deck is a no-brainer. Because Doom’s custom card mechanics often protect your own artifact creatures or allow you to rebuild rapidly from the graveyard, asymmetric board wipes like *Toxic Deluge* allow you to wipe out your opponents' boards while leaving your mechanical empire relatively unscathed.

## Залізний кулак Дума: перевидання "Toxic Deluge"

Роками чорні колоди у форматі Командир покладалися на кілька обраних «зачисток столу» (board wipes), щоб розчистити шлях до перемоги. Головною серед них є **Toxic Deluge** — заклинання (sorcery), яке дозволяє заплатити X життя, щоб дати всім істотам -X/-X до кінця ходу. Воно обходить незнищенність (indestructible), ігнорує оберіг (ward) і коштує всього три мани (одну чорну та дві безколірні). 

Історично склалося, що ціна на *Toxic Deluge* стабільно трималася в межах від $15 до $22 залежно від видання. Це абсолютний маст-хев (staple) формату, а отже, попит на нього завжди залишається надзвичайно високим. 

У майбутньому продукті з Доктором Думом карта *Toxic Deluge* отримає розкішне, лиходійське оновлення. Новий арт зображує Доктора Дума, який тріумфально стоїть над переможеними героями та механічними вартовими, оточений їдким смарагдово-зеленим хімічним туманом. Художній текст (flavor text) ідеально передає зарозумілу зверхність Дума. Для гравців, які обожнюють збирати тематичні колоди, це найкраща версія і без того ідеальної карти.
