## The Great Motherboard Clearance of 2025

For the last few years, building a high-end PC has felt like an exercise in financial pain. Between scalper-priced GPUs and inflation-driven component hikes, PC enthusiasts have grown used to compromising on their dream builds. But as we move through 2025, a bizarre and unprecedented anomaly has hit the PC hardware market.

Motherboard manufacturers are currently sitting on a mountain of unsold inventory. To clear out warehouse space, retailers are slashing prices on flagship Intel Z790 and AMD X670E motherboards by up to 57%. Boards that originally launched with eye-watering price tags of $500 to $700 are now sitting on virtual shelves at entry-level prices.

There is a catch, however. While you can buy a premium motherboard for pennies on the dollar, the global DRAM market is undergoing a massive supply squeeze. DDR5 RAM prices are surging due to manufacturers shifting their focus to AI enterprise hardware. If you want to build a PC right now, you need to know how to navigate this uneven market to get the absolute most performance for your money.

## Why Are Flagship Motherboards So Cheap?

To understand this market crash, we have to look at the transition between CPU generations. 

On the Intel side, the LGA1700 socket (which covers 12th, 13th, and 14th Gen Core processors) has officially reached the end of its life. With the arrival of Arrow Lake and the new LGA1851 socket, motherboard makers like ASUS, MSI, Gigabyte, and ASRock are desperate to liquidate their remaining Z790 inventory. Because these high-end Z790 boards were produced in massive quantities during the tail end of the chip shortage, supply has vastly outstripped demand.

On the AMD side, the AM5 platform is still going strong, but the launch of the X870 and X870E chipsets has forced retailers to aggressively discount older, yet incredibly capable, X670 and X670E motherboards. Structurally and functionally, a high-end X670E board offers almost identical performance, PCIe Gen 5 support, and robust power delivery compared to its newer X870E counterparts, making the older models an absolute steal.

## The Catch: DDR5 RAM Prices Are Surging

While motherboard prices are in a race to the bottom, memory prices are climbing a steep hill. 

The reason is simple: Artificial Intelligence. Major DRAM manufacturers like Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron have diverted a massive portion of their production capacity away from standard consumer DDR5 memory to produce High-Bandwidth Memory (HBM3e and HBM4) and high-density DDR5 server RAM for AI data centers. 

This shift has created a supply bottleneck for consumer-grade dual-channel DDR5 kits. Over the past six months, standard 32GB (2x16GB) DDR5-6000 memory kits have jumped in price by roughly 25% to 35%. If you aren't careful, the money you save on your motherboard could easily be swallowed up by overpaying for your system memory.

## Top Motherboard Deals to Grab Right Now

If you want to capitalize on this market imbalance, here are the best premium motherboard deals currently available. These boards offer overbuilt power delivery, premium audio codecs, high-speed networking, and gorgeous aesthetics for mid-range prices.

*   **Platform:** Intel LGA1700 (12th, 13th, & 14th Gen)

The MSI MEG Z790 ACE is an absolute titan of a motherboard. It features a massive 24+1+2 phase power delivery system, a full server-grade chest plate, dual Thunderbolt 4 ports, and five M.2 slots covered by heavy-duty heatsinks. At its original $699 price point, it was a tough sell for anyone but extreme overclockers. At under $300, it costs less than many mid-range Z790 boards did at launch, offering unparalleled premium build quality for a fraction of the cost.

### 2. Gigabyte X670E Aorus Master
*   **Original MSRP:** $499

*   **Platform:** AMD AM5 (Ryzen 7000, 8000, and 9000 Series)

For AMD builders, the X670E Aorus Master is one of the most well-rounded enthusiast boards ever made. It features a 16+2+2 phase VRM, support for multiple PCIe 5.0 M.2 SSDs, and 10GbE super-fast Ethernet. Because AMD has promised to support the AM5 socket through at least 2027, buying this discounted flagship now gives you an incredible foundation for future Ryzen CPU upgrades without needing to upgrade your motherboard.

### 3. ASRock Z790 Taichi Lite
*   **Original MSRP:** $379
*   **Current Street Price:** ~$229 (Save 40%)
*   **Platform:** Intel LGA1700

ASRock’s "Taichi Lite" concept was brilliant: take the ultra-premium PCB, VRM, and features of the flagship Taichi board, strip away the expensive RGB lighting and heavy decorative plastic armor, and sell it for cheaper. Now heavily discounted, the Z790 Taichi Lite offers a flagship 24-phase VRM, an ESS SABRE DAC for audiophile-grade onboard sound, and a USB4 port for just over $200. It is arguably the best value-for-money Intel board on the market today.

## How to Build Smart in This Weird Market

To make the most of this hardware landscape, you need a strategic approach to your component selection:

*   **Don't Overspend on RAM Speed:** Because DDR5 prices are high, avoid buying ultra-high-speed kits (like DDR5-7200 or DDR5-8000). For AMD Ryzen, DDR5-6000 CL30 remains the absolute sweet spot for stability and performance. For Intel, DDR5-6400 CL32 offers the best balance of price and performance.
*   **Reallocate Your Budget:** The $150 to $300 you save on a discounted flagship motherboard should be funneled directly into offsetting the higher RAM costs, or better yet, buying a faster GPU (like upgrading from an RTX 4070 to an RTX 4070 Ti Super).
*   **Buy for Future Upgrades:** If you go the AMD route, buying a discounted X670E flagship ensures you have PCIe Gen 5 support on both your primary GPU slot and your main M.2 slot, guaranteeing your system will be fully compatible with next-generation graphics cards and ultra-fast SSDs for years to come.

## Bottom Line: Our Verdict

The current state of the PC hardware market is highly unusual, but it represents a massive win for savvy system builders. 

While the rise in DDR5 RAM prices is annoying, the massive, unprecedented discounts on flagship motherboards more than make up for the difference. Buying a high-end motherboard isn't just about bragging rights; it means getting better power delivery for cooler-running components, higher-quality onboard audio, faster networking, and a much easier building experience thanks to premium toolless designs. 

If you have been waiting on the sidelines to upgrade your PC, now is the time to strike. Snag a heavily discounted flagship board like the **Gigabyte X670E Aorus Master** or **MSI MEG Z790 ACE** before warehouse inventories dry up for good.
