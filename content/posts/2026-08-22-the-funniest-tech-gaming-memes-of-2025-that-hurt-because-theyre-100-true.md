## 4. The 4 Stages of Modern PC Troubleshooting

Every tech enthusiast in 2025 follows the exact same descent into madness:

1. **Denial:** "It's just a driver issue. I'll clean install with DDU and everything will hit a stable 144 FPS in 4K again."
2. **Anger:** Scouring 8-year-old Reddit threads where the only reply is a deleted user saying "Nvm fixed it" with zero explanation.
