## The 44.8 Million Bottleneck: Bluesky's New Frontier

For the past two years, Bluesky has been the darling of the decentralized web. Born out of an internal Twitter project and powered by the open-source Authenticated Transfer (AT) Protocol, the platform witnessed explosive growth during periods of volatility on X (formerly Twitter). However, as we roll through 2025, the platform has hit a conspicuous development plateau. Hovering at approximately 44.8 million users, Bluesky's rapid hockey-stick growth has begun to stall.

To break through this ceiling, Bluesky is executing its most significant pivot yet: moving away from being just a public microblogging town square and transforming into an ecosystem of intimate spaces. The platform has officially rolled out direct group chats, private messaging enhancements, and algorithmic "Communities." 

Crucially, this transition relies heavily on a decentralized approach to artificial intelligence and automated moderation. Unlike Meta's centralized AI algorithms, Bluesky is leveraging user-curated AI labeling services and open-source models to keep these new community spaces clean and functional. 

## Why the Pivot to Group Chats and Communities Matters

Public microblogging is exhausting. One of the primary reasons users migrate away from platforms like X or Threads is the constant exposure to outrage, rage-baiting, and unprompted arguments. By launching Group Chats and Communities, Bluesky is trying to capture the cozy, high-retention vibe of platforms like Discord, Reddit, and WhatsApp.

In Bluesky’s new framework, "Communities" function as self-governing nodes. Users can join specific hubs dedicated to gaming, AI art, political theory, or local news. Within these hubs, group chats allow for real-time conversation. 

However, moderation in private group chats and decentralized communities is a technical nightmare. If Bluesky monitors everything, it violates its promise of decentralization. If it monitors nothing, these spaces risk becoming breeding grounds for spam, harassment, and illicit content. The solution? An innovative blend of open-source AI moderation tools and user-selected algorithmic feeds.

## The AI Connection: Decentralized Moderation and Custom Feeds

Bluesky’s secret weapon is **Ozone**, its open-source collaborative moderation tool. Ozone allows independent developers and community admins to run their own AI-assisted moderation pipelines. 

Instead of relying on a single corporate AI model (like Meta's Llama or Google's Gemini) to dictate what is permissible globally, Bluesky allows community admins to deploy custom AI classifiers. For example, a community dedicated to family-friendly gaming can run a lightweight, fine-tuned Llama model to automatically flag and filter out explicit language or toxic behavior in real-time group chats. 

Furthermore, Bluesky’s custom feed architecture relies heavily on machine learning algorithms that users can choose to subscribe to. If you want an AI-curated feed that only shows positive news, you can subscribe to it. If you want a raw, unfiltered chronological feed, that is your choice. This level of algorithmic agency is unprecedented in mainstream social media.

## Challenges Ahead: Can Decentralization Scale?

Despite the brilliant engineering behind the AT Protocol and Ozone, Bluesky faces an uphill battle. The average social media user does not care about decentralization, federated servers, or data portability. They care about where their friends are. 

With Meta's Threads boasting over 275 million monthly active users and X retaining its core base of political and media elites, Bluesky's 44.8 million users represent a highly dedicated but relatively niche audience. The success of this pivot relies on whether "Communities" can offer a demonstrably better, less toxic user experience than Reddit or Discord. If the AI-assisted moderation tools fail to curb spam bots and bad actors, users will quickly retreat back to walled gardens.

## Our Verdict

Bluesky’s pivot to group chats and communities is a necessary and highly strategic move for 2025. By giving users control over their own algorithms and moderation pipelines, Bluesky is proving that a healthier, user-owned social web is technically viable. 

For creators and brands, now is the time to build. While the audience is smaller than Threads, the engagement rates on Bluesky are remarkably high. By deploying smart AI tools like **Buffer** for scheduling and leveraging open-source models via **Groq** for custom community moderation, you can establish an active, safe, and highly loyal community before the platform inevitably breaks through its current growth ceiling.

## Чому перехід до групових чатів і спільнот має значення

Запускаючи групові чати та спільноти, Bluesky намагається відтворити затишну атмосферу з високим рівнем утримання користувачів, притаманну таким платформам, як Discord, Reddit і WhatsApp.

У новій структурі Bluesky «Спільноти» функціонують як самоврядні вузли. Користувачі можуть приєднуватися до конкретних хабів, присвячених геймінгу, ШІ-арту, політичній теорії чи місцевим новинам. Усередині цих хабів групові чати дозволяють спілкуватися в реальному часі.

Проте модерація в приватних групових чатах і децентралізованих спільнотах — це технічний кошмар. Якщо Bluesky контролюватиме все, це порушить обіцянку децентралізації. Якщо не контролюватиме нічого — ці простори ризикують перетворитися на розсадники спаму, цькування та незаконного контенту. Рішення?
