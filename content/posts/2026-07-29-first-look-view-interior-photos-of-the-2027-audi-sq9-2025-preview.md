## Introduction

For years, automotive enthusiasts and high-performance family haulers have whispered about the mythical flagship SUV from Ingolstadt: the Audi SQ9. In 2025, the long wait is finally coming to an end. Recent spy photos and internal design leaks give us our clearer-than-ever view inside the 2027 Audi SQ9. Positioning itself above the popular Q8/SQ8 lineup, this full-size three-row performance beast is designed to take direct aim at established heavyweights like the BMW X7 M60i and the Mercedes-AMG GLS 63.

While the exterior sports aggressive quad exhaust tips, an oversized single-frame grille, and sleek lighting elements, it is the cabin where Audi has truly poured its engineering genius. If you are curious about what driving a futuristic, V8-powered ultra-luxury cruiser feels like, let us dive deep into the cabin details unveiled in these latest interior photos.

## A New Era of Cabin Design: The Quad-Screen Layout

When inspecting the latest interior photos of the 2027 Audi SQ9, the first thing that strikes you is the sheer real estate dedicated to high-definition digital displays. Moving away from the dual-touchscreen setup found in current Audi models, the SQ9 introduces a continuous curved glass panel that houses both the Virtual Cockpit driver display and a massive central infotainment screen.

Key highlights from the leaked dashboard shots include:
- **14.5-inch OLED Central Touchscreen:** Powered by Audi's newest Android Automotive-based OS, offering seamless voice commands and fluid gesture control.
- **11.9-inch Digital Driver Display:** Configurable performance gauges, full-screen 3D satellite mapping, and real-time telemetry options.
- **Dedicated Front Passenger Display:** Positioned directly in front of the passenger seat, allowing video streaming, navigation input, and media controls without distracting the driver.
- **Augmented Reality Head-Up Display (AR-HUD):** Projects directional arrows, speed warnings, and driver assistance alerts directly onto the road ahead.

The layout maintains a remarkably clean, tactile, and uncluttered aesthetic. Minimalist capacitive touch buttons replace physical switchgear, though high-touch controls like the volume knob and drive-select toggles retain a satisfying aluminum feel.

## Three Rows of Supreme Comfort: Seating and Space

One of the main criticisms of the Audi SQ8 was its sacrifice of a third row for a coupe-like roofline. The 2027 Audi SQ9 resolves this entirely. Photos confirm a generous 2+2+2 captain’s chair configuration, alongside an optional 7-passenger bench layout.

The front seats are trimmed in rich Valcona leather featuring diamond-stitched quilting and illuminated 'S' badges embedded into the headrests. Multi-contour adjustment options include 18-way electric positioning, heated and ventilated cushions, and ten dynamic pneumatic massage programs. Meanwhile, second-row passengers are treated to individual climate zones, electric side window blinds, and dual 10.1-inch entertainment tablets mounted on the front seatbacks.

Crucially, the third row appears far more accommodating than that of its smaller siblings. Generous footwell depths and dedicated rear air vents ensure adults can ride comfortably over long distances.

## Ambient Lighting and Premium Sound

Audi has always mastered cabin mood lighting, and the SQ9 elevates this art form. Photos reveal dynamic light strips spanning the entire dashboard width and tracing the contours of the door panels. These light strips do not just look stunning—they respond to driving conditions, flashing red during blind-spot alerts or pulsing blue to match climate adjustment inputs.

Audio is handled by an available Bang & Olufsen 3D Advanced Sound System. Featuring 23 strategically placed speakers and active noise cancellation, the cabin isolates road noise effectively, allowing passengers to enjoy serene high-speed cruising.

## Три ряди найвищого комфорту: сидіння та простір

Одним із головних зауважень до Audi SQ8 була відмова від третього ряду на користь купеподібної лінії даху. Audi SQ9 2027 року повністю вирішує цю проблему. Фотографії підтверджують простору конфігурацію з капітанськими кріслами 2+2+2, а також опціональний 7-місний диван.

Передні сидіння оздоблені розкішною шкірою Valcona з ромбоподібним простроченням та підсвічуваними емблемами «S», вбудованими у підголівники. Варіанти мультиконтурного регулювання включають 18-позиційне електропривідне налаштування, підігрів, вентиляцію та десять динамічних пневматичних програм масажу. Тим часом пасажири другого ряду отримують індивідуальні кліматичні зони, електричні шторки на бокових вікнах та два 10,1-дюймові розважальні планшети, закріплені на спинках передніх сидінь.

Що важливо, третій ряд виглядає значно просторішим, ніж у менших братів по лінійці. Глибокі ніші для ніг та окремі дефлектори обдування гарантують, що дорослі зможуть комфортно подорожувати на далекі відстані.
