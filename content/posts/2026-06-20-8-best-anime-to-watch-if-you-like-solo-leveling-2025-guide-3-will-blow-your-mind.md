## Introduction

Solo Leveling took the anime and gaming world by storm, solidifying itself as a cultural phenomenon. Sung Jinwoo’s journey from the "Weakest Hunter of All Mankind" to an absolute god-tier shadow monarch captured the hearts of millions. With Solo Leveling Season 2 and the hit action-RPG *Solo Leveling: Arise* dominating 2025, fans are desperately searching for their next high-octane, system-based action fix.

If you love the thrill of leveling up, glowing blue status screens, dark fantasy aesthetics, and incredibly animated fight scenes, we have you covered. Here are the 8 best anime to watch if you love Solo Leveling.

---

## 1. Tower of God

If the webtoon origins of Solo Leveling are what caught your eye, *Tower of God* is your logical next step. The story follows Bam, a young boy who enters a mysterious, colossal Tower to find his childhood friend, Rachel. 

Like Solo Leveling, the world is governed by strict rules, tests, and an overwhelming power hierarchy. The action is stylized, the lore is incredibly deep, and the climb up the tower feels exactly like clearing increasingly difficult dungeon gates.

## 2. Shangri-La Frontier

For those who love the gaming mechanics of Solo Leveling, *Shangri-La Frontier* is a masterpiece of modern gaming anime. It follows Sunraku, a "trash-game hunter" who decides to tackle a god-tier VRMMO. 

While it is lighter in tone than Jinwoo's dark journey, the fight choreography is arguably some of the best in the entire anime industry. The bosses look like they stepped straight out of a Dark Souls game, and the progression system feels incredibly rewarding to watch.

## 3. The Eminence in Shadow (The Mind-Blower)

**Prepare to have your mind blown.** If you love Solo Leveling because of Jinwoo's dark, edgy, "I am the shadows" aesthetic, *The Eminence in Shadow* takes that exact concept, turns it up to eleven, and injects a brilliant dose of dark comedy.

Cid Kagenou is reincarnated into a fantasy world where he wants nothing more than to play the role of a mysterious mastermind operating from the shadows. He creates a fictional cult to fight—except, unbeknownst to him, the cult is actually real. Cid is absurdly, hilariously overpowered, and his shadow-based magic attacks (including his famous "I am Atomic" blast) will give you the exact same goosebumps as Jinwoo summoning his shadow army.

## 4. Arifureta: From Commonplace to World's Strongest

*Arifureta* is the ultimate "betrayed in a dungeon and must survive" story. Hajime Nagumo is betrayed by his classmates and plunged into the deepest depths of an abyss. To survive, he must abandon his naive nature, eat monsters to gain their skills, and craft high-tech weaponry using transmutation magic.

It mirrors the early "Double Dungeon" arc of Solo Leveling perfectly. Hajime goes from a weak, fragile boy to a white-haired, gun-toting badass who takes no prisoners.

## 5. Sword Art Online

We cannot talk about "System" anime without paying respects to the pioneer. *Sword Art Online* trapped thousands of players in a death game where dying in-game meant dying in real life. 

Kirito’s journey as a solo player fighting to clear 100 floors of Aincrad paved the way for the entire modern LitRPG and progression fantasy genre. If you want high-stakes boss fights and a legendary dual-wielding protagonist, this is a classic that still holds up.

## 6. Hunter x Hunter (2011)

Solo Leveling owes a massive debt to *Hunter x Hunter*. The entire concept of "Hunters," ranked guilds, and chimera-like monsters threatening humanity was popularized by Yoshihiro Togashi’s masterpiece.

Following Gon and Killua as they navigate the dangerous Hunter Exam and unlock the complex power system of Nen is an absolute joy. The Chimera Ant arc, in particular, features some of the darkest, most intense battles in anime history.

## 7. That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime

If you love the aspect of Jinwoo building his Shadow Army and managing his skills, *TenSura* is a must-watch. Rimuru Tempest is reincarnated as a lowly slime but quickly acquires the "Great Sage" skill, allowing him to absorb other creatures and analyze their abilities.

Rimuru slowly builds a nation of monsters, leveling them up, naming them, and defending his territory from hostile forces. It is the perfect blend of cozy nation-building and jaw-dropping, overpowered action.

## 8. The King's Avatar (Quan Zhi Gao Shou)

For gamers who want a more realistic take on esports and gaming dominance, this Chinese donghua is spectacular. Ye Xiu, a legendary pro-player of the MMORPG *Glory*, is forced to retire and surrender his account. Undeterred, he starts fresh on a brand-new server as a casual internet cafe employee.

Using his god-tier knowledge of the game's mechanics, he absolutely dominates the server under a new alias. The animation is incredibly fluid, making the keyboard-clicking, high-APM gaming action feel like a high-stakes fantasy battle.
