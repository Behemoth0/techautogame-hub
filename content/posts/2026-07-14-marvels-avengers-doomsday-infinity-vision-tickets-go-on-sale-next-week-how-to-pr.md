## What is the Infinity Vision Experience?

For those unfamiliar, **Infinity Vision** is a cutting-edge theatrical format utilizing proprietary 16K laser projection systems, dynamic active haptic seating, and a customized 360-degree spatial audio array. Developed in collaboration with top-tier gaming sound designers and visual effects artists, the format aims to make viewers feel like they are standing directly on the battlefield alongside Earth's Mightiest Heroes.

Reports indicate that the Infinity Vision screenings will also feature exclusive interactive pre-shows, allowing audiences to use mobile devices or companion apps to interact with the environment before the film begins. It is a massive step forward in blending cinema with real-time gaming technology, making it a must-watch for tech enthusiasts and gamers alike.
