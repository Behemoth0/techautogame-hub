## How to Set Up and Use Claude’s Voice Mode: Step-by-Step

Getting started with Claude’s voice feature is straightforward across iOS, Android, and web desktop environments. Follow these simple steps to activate and start chatting:

### Step 1: Update Your Mobile App or Browser
Ensure you have downloaded the latest version of the Claude app from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. If you are using a desktop, ensure your browser (Chrome, Safari, or Edge) is updated. Voice features are continuously upgraded, so running the latest 2025 build ensures access to low-latency processing and background noise reduction.

### Step 2: Launch the Voice Interface
Open the Claude app on your mobile device or open your desktop session. Right beside the main text input prompt bar at the bottom of the screen, you will spot a distinct **Waveform/Microphone Icon**. Tap or click this icon to initiate a live conversational voice session.

### Step 3: Grant System Permissions
If it is your first time using the feature, your device operating system will prompt you to grant microphone access. Allow permissions so Claude can receive high-fidelity audio input.

### Step 4: Choose Your Preferred Voice Profile
In the app settings menu (accessible via your profile icon in the top corner), navigate to **Voice Settings**. Anthropic offers several distinct voice personas, ranging from calm and analytical to dynamic and conversational. Pick the option that best fits your workflow.

### Step 5: Start Speaking Naturally
Once the connection indicator lights up, simply speak. You do not need to repeat wake words like "Hey Claude" before every phrase. Talk naturally, pause when thinking, and feel free to interrupt the AI if it strays off-topic—Claude will dynamically adjust to your conversational flow.
