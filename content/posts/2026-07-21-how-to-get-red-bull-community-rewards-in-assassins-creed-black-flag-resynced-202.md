## Ahoy, Captains: The Red Bull x AC Black Flag Resynced Event

Ubisoft has officially brought back one of the most beloved entries in stealth-action history with Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced. Rebuilt from the ground up for modern hardware in 2025, Edward Kenway's high-seas adventure features stunning ray-traced Caribbean waters, reworked naval combat mechanics, and full cross-platform progression. To celebrate the launch, Ubisoft has teamed up with Red Bull for a global promotional campaign that gives players access to exclusive cosmetics, powerful early-game ship upgrades, and community-wide XP multipliers.

Whether you are aiming to hoist the legendary Crimson Wings Sail on the Jackdaw or want to boost your crew's naval prowess during the early hours of the campaign, participating in the Red Bull Community Rewards event is one of the best ways to hit the ocean running. In this guide, we break down everything you need to know about unlocking these limited-time rewards, redeeming your ring-pull codes, and maximizing your gains on the high seas.

## Step-by-Step: How to Unlock Red Bull Rewards

Getting your hands on the promotional loot requires a few simple steps. The promotional cans are rolling out worldwide, featuring distinct Black Flag Resynced branding on the packaging.

1. **Purchase Promotional Red Bull Cans:** Look for specially marked 12 oz or 16 oz Red Bull Energy Drink cans in store aisles. These feature Edward Kenway on the exterior wrap.
2. **Find Your Unique Code:** Pop the top and check the underside of the silver ring-pull tab. You will find an 8-character alphanumeric code stamped directly onto the aluminum.
3. **Visit the Redemption Portal:** Go to the official Ubisoft promo page at `redbull.com/acblackflag` on your mobile device or desktop browser.
4. **Log In to Ubisoft Connect:** Sign into the Ubisoft Connect account linked to your platform of choice (PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S, or PC).
5. **Enter Your Codes:** Type in your 8-character tab code and click redeem. Your rewards will automatically deliver to your in-game inventory upon your next login.

## Exclusive Red Bull Rewards Breakdown

Ubisoft and Red Bull have structured the promo into tiered individual unlocks alongside a global community goal system:

* **Code 1:** Crimson Wings Jackdaw Sail Scheme & Red Bull Figurehead
* **Code 2:** Energy Cutlass Set (Dual-wield swords with electro-spark particle effects)
* **Code 3:** 2x Community XP Booster (24-hour real-time duration)
* **Code 4+:** 500 Sugar Cane & 500 Wood Crafting Bundles per additional code redeemed

In addition to individual tier unlocks, every code redeemed contributes to the global **Resynced High Seas Community Meter**. Once the community collectively redeems 5 million codes worldwide, all active players unlock the exclusive *Apex Predator Naval Armor Set* for the Jackdaw, regardless of how many individual codes they personally submitted.

## Troubleshooting Code Redemption Issues

If you encounter errors while submitting your tab codes, try these common troubleshooting methods:

* **Double Check Letters vs Numbers:** It is easy to confuse the letter 'O' with the number '0', or 'I' with '1'. Make sure you enter characters carefully.
* **Clear Browser Cache:** If the submission button hangs, clear your browser cookies or use an Incognito/Private browsing window.
* **Verify Linked Accounts:** Ensure your Ubisoft Connect profile is correctly linked to your current console ID before redeeming codes; rewards cannot be transferred across separate accounts once claimed.
