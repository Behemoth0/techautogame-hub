## Introduction

When Santa Monica Studio wrapped up the Norse duology with *God of War Ragnarök* and its brilliant *Valhalla* DLC, fans were left marveling at one of the greatest redemption arcs in video game history. Kratos evolved from a rage-fueled monster into a revered god of hope. Yet, despite the narrative closure, a giant shadow still hangs over the entire Norse saga: Laufey the Just, better known as Faye.

Faye is the catalyst for everything. Her death triggers the journey in the 2018 reboot, and her past actions lay the tracks for Kratos and Atreus’s destiny. Despite her immense importance, we only ever see her in brief, dreamlike flashbacks. As we look ahead to the gaming landscape of 2025, rumors of a standalone *God of War* spinoff focusing on Faye continue to gather steam. Such a game wouldn't just be a fantastic action-adventure; it could finally answer the biggest mystery left in the series.

## The Great Mystery: How Did Faye Foresee Everything?

Throughout *God of War* (2018) and *Ragnarök*, we learn that Faye possessed a startlingly precise knowledge of the future. She painted the yellow markers that guided Kratos and Atreus across the realms, predicted their battles, and knew exactly how the twilight of the Norse gods would play out. 

But how? While the Giants were known for their foresight, Faye’s predictions were remarkably granular. More importantly, the Norse saga hints at a darker, more complex side to her character. Was she simply a loving mother protecting her family, or was she a master tactician who used her husband and son as weapons of vengeance against Odin? 

A spinoff focusing on Faye’s life before she met Kratos could explore her rebellion against the Aesir. It would show us her perspective on the Giants' extinction, her relationship with the Huldra brothers (Brok and Sindri), and her ultimate decision to lay down her shield and live as a mortal. Most excitingly, it could depict her legendary, realm-shaking battle against Thor in Vanaheim—a duel so fierce it left a frozen lightning bolt standing as a monument to their clash.

## Gameplay Potential: A Different Kind of God of War

Playing as Faye would offer a thrilling departure from Kratos’s brutal, heavy combat style. Before she gifted it to Kratos, Faye was the original wielder of the Leviathan Axe. Forged by Brok and Sindri to rival Thor's Mjölnir, the axe was designed specifically for her.

An adventure starring Faye could blend the satisfying, frosty hacks of the Leviathan Axe with agile Giant magic (Jötnar magic). Where Kratos relies on raw, Spartan strength, Faye could utilize environmental manipulation, illusions, and rapid-fire runic attacks. A prequel would also give us a chance to explore the realms during the height of Odin's tyranny, offering a bleak, war-torn aesthetic that contrasts with the rebuilding worlds we saw at the end of *Ragnarök*.

## Bottom Line / Our Verdict

While Kratos's story has reached a beautiful, peaceful plateau, the *God of War* universe is far too rich to abandon. A spinoff starring Faye is the perfect way for Santa Monica Studio to bridge the gap before the next major mainline entry. It would allow players to experience the Norse realms from a completely fresh perspective, wield the Leviathan Axe with a different combat flair, and finally resolve the mysteries of the Giants' prophecies. For any fan of the franchise, a Faye-centric prequel isn't just a want—it is a narrative necessity.
