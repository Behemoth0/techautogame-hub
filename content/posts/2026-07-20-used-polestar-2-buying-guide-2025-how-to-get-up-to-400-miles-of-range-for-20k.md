## Introduction: The Used EV Bargain of the Decade

If you have been watching the automotive market over the last couple of years, you will know that electric vehicle depreciation has been brutal for first owners, but an absolute goldmine for used buyers. In 2025, this trend has reached a fascinating tipping point. A car that once commanded a premium of over £50,000 is now sitting on used forecourts for less than the price of a basic, entry-level petrol hatchback.

Enter the **Polestar 2**. 

With its sharp Scandinavian styling, robust Volvo-derived build quality, and class-leading Android Automotive infotainment system, the Polestar 2 has always been one of our favorite premium EVs. Now that early models have comfortably dipped below the £20,000 mark—and even the highly efficient, facelifted longer-range versions are tantalizingly close to that threshold—it represents one of the smartest used purchases you can make today. Here is our comprehensive buying guide to securing a Polestar 2 with up to 400 miles of range for around £20k.

---

## Understanding the Polestar 2 Range: FWD vs. RWD

To find the right Polestar 2, you need to understand the crucial update that occurred for the **2024 Model Year (MY24)**, which began deliveries in late 2023. 

Before this update, single-motor Polestar 2s were front-wheel drive (FWD). While competent, they suffered from occasional traction issues under heavy acceleration. The MY24 facelift changed everything: the single-motor variants switched to **rear-wheel drive (RWD)**, received much more efficient motors, and got a larger 82kWh battery (up from 78kWh) for the Long Range version. This pushed the official WLTP range of the **Long Range Single Motor** up to an astonishing **406 miles**.

While these post-facelift RWD models are just starting to trickle down toward the £21,000–£23,000 mark as ex-fleet cars flood the market in 2025, the pre-facelift FWD Long Range models (with a still-respectable 335-mile WLTP range) can easily be found for well under £19,000.

---

## Top 4 Used Polestar 2 & Alternative Recommendations

To help you navigate the market, we have highlighted the best used variants to look for right now, along with a key alternative to keep you informed.

### 1. The Range King: 2023/2024 Polestar 2 Long Range Single Motor (RWD Facelift)
*   **Approximate Used Price:** £21,500 - £23,500
*   **Why buy it:** This is the version that delivers the headline-grabbing 406 miles of WLTP range (expect around 310-330 miles in real-world mixed driving). It features the refreshed "SmartZone" front grille and faster 205kW DC charging. If you can stretch your budget slightly past £20k, this is the absolute sweet spot for highway cruisers.

### 2. The Smart Value Pick: 2021/2022 Polestar 2 Long Range Single Motor (FWD)
*   **Approximate Used Price:** £17,500 - £19,500
*   **Why buy it:** If £20k is your absolute hard ceiling, this model offers the best balance of price and usability. With a 78kWh battery delivering a WLTP range of 335 miles (around 250-270 real-world miles), it handles daily commutes and long road trips with ease without breaking the bank.

### 3. The Performance Bargain: 2021 Polestar 2 Long Range Dual Motor (with Performance Pack)
*   **Approximate Used Price:** £19,000 - £21,000
*   **Why buy it:** Want speed? The Dual Motor version packs 408 bhp and a 0-60 mph time of just 4.5 seconds. Look for models equipped with the gold-accented Performance Pack, which adds adjustable Öhlins dampers, Brembo brakes, and 20-inch forged alloy wheels. The range drops to around 298 miles WLTP, but the driving thrills are unmatched at this price point.

### 4. The Key Alternative: 2021 Tesla Model 3 Long Range AWD
*   **Approximate Used Price:** £18,000 - £20,000
*   **Why buy it:** The Polestar’s arch-nemesis. While the Model 3 offers superior charging infrastructure via the Tesla Supercharger network and slightly better software integration, it lacks the Polestar’s hatchback practicality, rugged build quality, and handsome, understated road presence.

---

## What to Look For When Buying a Used Polestar 2

While the Polestar 2 is generally a highly reliable vehicle built on Volvo’s CMA platform, there are a few specific areas you should inspect before handing over your cash:

### 1. The TCAM (Telematics and Connectivity Antenna Module) Issue
Early Polestar 2 models were notorious for TCAM glitches, which could cause the GPS, cellular connection, and key fob to temporarily stop working. Ensure the vehicle you are looking at has had its software updated to the latest OTA (Over-The-Air) version, which has resolved this issue for the vast majority of owners.

### 2. Suspension and Ride Comfort
Be aware that the Polestar 2 is set up on the firmer side. If you are looking at a model with the **Performance Pack**, the manually adjustable Öhlins dampers can be set to softer levels, but they will still feel stiff to some. Take a test drive over rough urban tarmac to ensure you are comfortable with the ride quality.

### 3. Interior Wear and Materials
Many Polestar 2s come with "WeaveTech" vegan upholstery. While durable and water-resistant, early versions of this material were prone to staining from sweat or spilled water. Check the seat bolsters for discoloration or cracking.

### 4. Battery Health and Charging History
Ask the dealer or seller for a battery health report if possible. While EV battery degradation is generally much lower than early adopters feared, ensuring the car hasn't spent its entire life sitting at 100% charge on a rapid DC charger will give you peace of mind.

---

## Bottom Line / Our Verdict

In 2025, the used car market has handed buyers an extraordinary opportunity. The Polestar 2 is a beautifully built, premium, and distinctively designed electric fastback that bypasses the "tech-toy" aesthetic of Tesla in favor of genuine Scandinavian luxury.

If you can stretch your budget to **£21,500**, hunting down a **2023/2024 RWD Long Range Single Motor** will net you a modern EV with a borderline-revolutionary 400+ miles of WLTP range. However, even if you stick firmly to a **£18,000** budget, a pre-facelift Long Range model offers better build quality, style, and software than almost any brand-new EV you can buy today. It is, without a doubt, one of the best automotive bargains of the year.
