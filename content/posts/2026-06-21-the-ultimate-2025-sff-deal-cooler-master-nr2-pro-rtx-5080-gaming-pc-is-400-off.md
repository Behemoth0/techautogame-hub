## Why SFF Liquid Cooling Matters

Many prebuilt manufacturers throw high-end components into small cases with inadequate cooling, leading to thermal throttling where your expensive GPU runs at half-speed to avoid melting. 

Cooler Master avoids this pitfall entirely. The NR2 Pro utilizes a dual-chamber layout that isolates the GPU from the motherboard and CPU. The CPU is cooled by a custom 280mm All-in-One (AIO) liquid cooler, venting hot air directly out of the top of the chassis. The RTX 5080 draws fresh air directly from the side panels. This means you get full-tower thermal performance in a chassis that is roughly the size of a shoebox.
